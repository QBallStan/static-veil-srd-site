Most of what wears a body down comes through [[Conditions#Fatigue|Fatigue]]: −5 per stack, and at 4 stacks you're close to collapse. Where it comes from decides how it goes away.

## Holding Your Breath

A character can hold their breath for **Fortitude AR × 3 rounds** (at least 1), or half that while fighting, swimming hard or otherwise straining. After that, they make a [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Vigor]] check every round, at a cumulative −10. On a failure, they fall [[Conditions#Unconscious|Unconscious]].

Someone left Unconscious without air dies after **1d6 minutes**. Once they can breathe again, a First Aid test brings them round.

## Sleep

Every 24 hours without sleep gives 1 Fatigue. A full night's sleep removes 2 of those stacks.

## Food and Water

Each day without water gives 1 Fatigue, and so does every 3 days without food. Each day with enough to eat and drink removes 1 of those stacks.

## Exertion

For each hour of running, forced marching, heavy labor or other hard effort, make a Vigor check. On a failure, gain 1 Fatigue. An hour of rest removes 1 of those stacks.
