## Falling

A fall deals **2 damage for each meter beyond the first 2**. Grit reduces it, but Armor doesn't. A character who sees it coming can make an **Athletics** test to land well: each Hit takes 1 meter off the fall. Roll for Hit Location as normal. A soft landing, such as water, an awning or a pile of rubbish bags, can halve the damage, at the GM's discretion.

| Height | Damage |
|---|---:|
| 3 m (one floor) | 2 |
| 6 m (two floors) | 8 |
| 10 m (three floors) | 16 |
| 20 m | 36 |

## Drowning and Suffocation

Water, a sealed room, a lack of oxygen: use the rules for [[Endurance#Holding Your Breath|holding your breath]]. **Thick smoke** counts as having no air at all.

## Fire

Flames in the scenery work like a Fire attack, with no attack roll. A character who goes through or stays in them rolls each round for [[Damage Types and Effects#Fire|ignition]], usually at **5+** (sustained contact with flames). If they catch fire, they gain [[Conditions#Burning|Burning]].

## Electricity

Touching a live current deals damage with no attack roll. Its Bonus Dice always count, Grit reduces it, and Armor doesn't, unless it's insulated. It also has the [[Damage Types and Effects#Electric|Electric]] effect.

| Source | Damage |
|---|---|
| Household | 3 + 2d6 |
| Industrial | 6 + 3d6 |
| High voltage | 10 + 4d6 |

## Toxic Atmospheres

Gas, chemical fumes, industrial vapors: a [[Endurance#Holding Your Breath|held breath]] or a respirator keeps you safe. Otherwise, make a [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Vigor]] check each round. On a failure, you suffer the substance's effect: Fatigue, damage, Unconscious or whatever it lists (see [[Toxins and Disease]]).
