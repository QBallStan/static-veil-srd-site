Bullets aren't the only thing that can kill you. The acid rain eats through cheap filters, the stairwell is full of smoke, the extraction was supposed to happen an hour ago and nobody has slept in two days. This chapter is about what the world does to a body that keeps going anyway: exhaustion, hunger, thirst, air you can't breathe, and the fall you didn't plan for.
