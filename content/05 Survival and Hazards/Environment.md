The world in 2092 is rarely kind: acid rain, smog, power cuts, server rooms like ovens, winters that take anyone left outside.

## Visibility

Anything that relies on sight, including attacks, Perception and Stealth against someone watching, takes a penalty when it's hard to see:

| Conditions | Modifier |
|---|---:|
| **Dim:** night in the city, a dark corridor, light rain | −10 |
| **Dark or obscured:** an unlit room, thick smoke, heavy rain or snow, dense fog | −20 |
| **Complete darkness** | Sight is impossible |

If several apply, use the worst one. Low-Light Vision, Darkvision, Thermal Vision, Traits and gear ignore some or all of these penalties, as they say.

## Heat and Cold

Extreme heat or cold without protection calls for a [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Vigor]] check at each interval. On a failure, the character gains 1 [[Conditions#Fatigue|Fatigue]].

| Exposure | Interval | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Harsh** | Every hour | Desert at noon, a freezing night, an overheated industrial floor |
| **Severe** | Every 10 minutes | A blizzard, a sweltering engine room, icy water |
| **Extreme** | Every round | Next to a furnace, immersion in freezing water |

The right gear, such as insulated clothing, a cooling suit or an environmental mask, makes the check Easy, or removes it. An hour of shelter removes 1 stack of Fatigue from exposure.

## Weather

Weather mostly works through [[Modifiers|modifiers]] and the rules above:
- Rain and snow affect visibility.
- Ice and deep snow are [[Movement and Positioning#Difficult Terrain|Difficult Terrain]].
- Strong wind gives −10 to thrown and long-range attacks.
