A gun is only as good as what it fires. Standard rounds are cheap and predictable. Specialist ammunition trades reliability, penetration, range or price for an effect tailored to a target. Explosives are less forgiving: grenades, mines and charges solve problems fast, but they're hard to use quietly, dangerous to carry around and illegal almost everywhere.

## Ammunition

Ammunition is bought separately from the weapon, and tracked round by round. Magazines, drums, clips and belts hold it, but they aren't ammunition themselves.

Special ammunition can change the weapon's Damage, [[Active and Bonus Defense#Damage Reduction|ArP]] or Range, add an [[Damage Types and Effects#Elemental Effects|Elemental Effect]], or do something else listed in its entry. Anything it doesn't change uses the weapon's normal profile.

Rather than real-world calibers, ammunition comes in broad types. Weapons that use the same type share ammunition, unless an entry says otherwise.

| Type | Used by |
|---|---|
| Light Cartridges | Holdouts and other compact guns |
| Medium Cartridges | Service pistols, revolvers, SMGs, pistol-caliber carbines |
| Heavy Cartridges | Heavy pistols, big revolvers, large-caliber carbines |
| Assault Cartridges | Assault rifles, carbines, light machine guns |
| Long-Range Cartridges | Full-power rifles, precision rifles, general-purpose machine guns |
| Large Cartridges | Anti-materiel rifles, heavy machine guns |
| Shells | Shotguns |

Bows use arrows, crossbows use bolts, blowguns use darts, and slings use sling ammunition.

## Capacity

A weapon's Capacity gives how much it holds and how it's reloaded: **15(m)** is 15 rounds in a detachable magazine, and **6(cy)** is a six-round cylinder.

| Code | Feed | Code | Feed |
|:---:|---|:---:|---|
| m | Detachable magazine | i | Internal magazine |
| d | Detachable drum | r | Loaded round by round |
| c | Clip | pc | Power cell |
| b | Break action | fc | Fuel canister |
| belt | Belt | — | Can't be reloaded |
| cy | Cylinder | | |

Two weapons that use the same ammunition don't necessarily take the same magazines. Reloading costs are in [[Firearms#Reloading|Firearms]].

## Grenades

Grenades are compact explosive or dispersal devices that are thrown, launched or placed by hand. Each one lists its payload: explosive damage, an Elemental Effect, smoke, gas, a flash, electronic interference or something else. A grenade is used up when it goes off, so its Price and Availability are per grenade.

## Mines and Charges

**Mines** go off when something triggers them: proximity, pressure, a command signal, a tripwire, a sensor. **Charges** are placed for demolition, breaching or sabotage. Each lists its Damage, ArP, area, trigger and anything special. Placing, disarming or setting one up calls for a **Demolitions** test when the result is uncertain.

Throwing, areas of effect and dodging explosions are covered in [[Area Effects and Explosives]].
