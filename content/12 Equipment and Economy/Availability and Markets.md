Having the money doesn't mean you can find what you're after. Some gear is on every shelf. Some takes a specialist dealer, a corporate contact or a criminal one, or just time. Each item has an **Availability**, which is how hard it is to find, and an **Access Tag**, which is whether it's legal. The two are separate. An item can be easy to find and illegal to own, or perfectly legal and almost impossible to find.

## Availability

| Availability | Rarity |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Ubiquitous: on every shelf |
| 1–2 | Common |
| 3–4 | Uncommon |
| 5–7 | Rare |
| 8–10 | Exceptional |
| 11–14 | Exotic |

Listed Availability assumes a wealthy, developed economy. The GM can raise or lower it for local scarcity, isolation or disrupted supply.

## Sourcing

Anything with Availability 0 can be bought wherever there's a market for it. Anything higher has to be **sourced**. Make a **Sourcing Test** with whatever fits your approach: **Investigation** to dig through listings and trace stock, **Influence** to work your way through dealers, **Con** to use false credentials or hide what you want it for, **Streetwise** to ask the right people in the wrong places, or any other Skill that makes sense. You find a seller if your [[Success Targets and Hits#Hits|Hits]] reach the item's Availability.

A search takes **a day**. Before rolling, you can choose to spend longer: each extra day lowers the item's Availability by 1, up to 6 extra days. If that brings it to 0, you find a seller without rolling.

On a failure, nothing turns up, and you can't try again for that item for a week, unless something changes: another city, a new lead, another Contact. A [[Complications|Complication]] brings an unreliable seller, unwanted attention, a lost deposit or some other problem that fits.

**Contacts:** a [[Contacts|Contact]] who deals in that kind of gear can make the Sourcing Test for you, at **Competence × 10** with **Connection** [[Bonus Dice]].

**Illegal** gear can only be found through someone who deals in it: a Contact, or a dealer you've met through the story.


## Access Tags

| Access Tag | Code | Meaning |
|---|:---:|---|
| Legal | — | Anyone can buy, own and carry it. |
| Restricted | R | Legal to own, but carrying it is reasonable suspicion. The police can stop and search you, and corporate security will want a word, or ask you to hand it over while you're on their premises. How strict that is depends on where you are. |
| Illegal | X | You can't buy it openly, and you can't be caught with it. |

Legal is the default and isn't marked on profiles. The tags in the catalogues are a baseline: laws vary, and the GM can change a tag to fit where the campaign is set.
