There's no encumbrance system. Characters carry whatever they could realistically manage. Items still list a Weight, for when it matters how much is being moved: hauling a crate, loading a vehicle, lifting something heavy.

That doesn't mean what a character carries never matters. It shows up in the fiction instead of in a number. Someone walking through the city with three guns, tactical gear, breaching tools and a backpack full of explosives will be noticed: by the police, corporate security, gangs, anyone with a reason to look. If a loadout stops being believable, the GM steps in.
