All money in 2092 is digital. When you need to pay off the grid, you carry it on a **credchip**: a secure chip holding verified tokens. Whoever holds the chip holds the money, just like cash.

## Yuwan

**Yuwan (¥¥)** is the world's standard currency, and every price in these rules is in Yuwan. It's built for stability, not speculation: it's backed by broad reserves and accepted almost everywhere. On the street it's *dubs* or *twins*.

## Cryptos

**Cryptos** are volatile currencies, and the reason to check how you're being paid. A client who promised ¥¥10,000 can hand over ¥¥10,000 in Crypto at today's quoted rate, and by the time you cash it, it might be worth a quarter of that.

When a character is paid in Crypto, roll 1d10 and convert it to Yuwan straight away:

| 1d10 | Actual value |
|---:|---:|
| 1 | 25% |
| 2–3 | 50% |
| 4–6 | 75% |
| 7–9 | 100% |
| 10 | 150% |

From then on it's just Yuwan. Nobody tracks Crypto separately.

## Scripts

**Scripts** are money issued by a corporation. Most are pegged to the Yuwan at 1 to 1, but they're only worth that where they're accepted. The issuing corporation and its businesses take them at full value. Everyone else takes them at a discount, or not at all, especially rival corporations and independent markets. Exchanging them for Yuwan costs whatever the market will bear. ¥¥5,000 in Scripts isn't ¥¥5,000 you can spend.

## Buying and Selling

You can buy anything available at its listed price. Prices and [[Availability and Markets#Availability|Availability]] assume a wealthy, developed economy. In a poor, isolated or unstable area, the GM can adjust them.

Price, Availability and legality are separate things. Something expensive isn't necessarily hard to find, and something illegal isn't necessarily expensive. [[Availability and Markets#Access Tags|Restricted or Illegal]] goods may need sourcing (see [[Availability and Markets]]).

There's no standard resale value. What used, stolen, damaged or modified gear is worth depends on the buyer.
