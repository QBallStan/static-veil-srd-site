In 2092, weapons are everywhere, but not everyone gets the same ones. A cheap street pistol, a corporate security carbine, a century-old collector's piece and a restricted military rifle can all kill just as well. What sets them apart is who can get hold of them, where you can carry them, how easily they hide, and what it takes to keep them running. A weapon's stats say what it does once the shooting starts. Its [[Ammunition and Explosives#Ammunition|ammunition]], [[Concealment|Bulk]], [[Availability and Markets|Availability and Access Tag]] and mods say how practical it is the rest of the time.

## Weapon Traits

A weapon only has the Traits listed in its entry.

- **Minimum Strength X:** you need a [[Attributes#Attribute Rating|Strength AR]] of X to use the weapon. Below that, you can't use it. A securely mounted weapon ignores this Trait.
- **[[Melee Combat#Parry|Parry]] X:** when the weapon is used for a melee [[Active and Bonus Defense#Active Defense|Active Defense]], add X Bonus Dice to the roll. Like all defense dice, they always count.
- **Thrown:** it's built and balanced for throwing, and uses the [[Ranged Combat#Thrown Weapons|thrown weapon]] rules.
- **Two-Handed:** it needs both hands.

Other Traits may appear in individual entries.

## Improvised Weapons

Anything not made to be a weapon can still be swung at someone, at −10. The GM picks a profile:

| Improvised weapon | Examples | Damage |
|---|---|---|
| Light | Bottle, wrench, tool handle | 0 + 2d6 |
| Medium | Crowbar, chair leg, heavy tool | 1 + 2d6 |
| Heavy | Fire extinguisher, toolbox, a chunk of machinery | 2 + 2d6 |

Improvised weapons have **ArP 0**, unless the object is especially good at piercing or cutting. Fragile ones may bend, break or shatter after a solid hit.

## Firearms

How firearms shoot ([[Firearms#Shot Rate|Shot Rate]], reloading) is covered in [[Firearms]]. Here are two things specific to each gun.

**Recoil Modifier:** each firearm adjusts the shooter's [[Sub-Attributes#Recoil Control|Recoil Control]] for that weapon: **effective Recoil Control = Recoil Control + Recoil Modifier**. Shot Rate only gives its bonus up to that limit. Hard-kicking guns usually hit hard too, but in weak hands they can push effective Recoil Control below 0. When that happens, the shooter takes −5 for each point below 0 on every shot, even at SR0.

**Capacity:** how much ammunition it holds, and how it's reloaded (see [[Ammunition and Explosives#Magazines and Power Cells|Magazines and Power Cells]]).

Mods are covered in [[Firearm Modding]].
