
[[Weapons#Firearms|Firearms]] can be customized with modifications that alter their function, handling, electronics, accessories, or other capabilities.

Each firearm belongs to a **Frame** that determines which modification slots it has. Each available slot can hold one modification.

Having the correct slot does not automatically mean a modification is compatible. Individual modifications list any Frame requirements, weapon requirements, installation procedure, and other restrictions that apply.

Purely cosmetic changes do not normally use modification slots unless they provide a mechanical benefit.

## Firearm Frames

Every firearm belongs to one of three Frames. A firearm's Frame determines which modification slots it has.

| Frame | Internal | Top | Bottom | Muzzle | Stock |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Small | ✓ |  |  |  |  |
| Handgun | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |  |
| Full-Size | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |

Each firearm has one slot of each type shown for its Frame.

**Small** firearms include holdouts and similarly compact designs.

**Handgun** firearms include pistols, revolvers, machine pistols, and similar weapons.

**Full-Size** firearms include SMGs, shotguns, rifles, machine guns, and similar weapons.

A firearm's individual entry may override its normal Frame when its construction requires it.

## Modification Slots

Firearm modification slots represent the part of the weapon being altered or fitted with additional equipment.

**Internal:** Changes to the firearm's internal mechanisms, electronics, feed system, fire-control system, or similar components.

**Top:** Optics and sighting systems such as scopes, reflex sights, thermal optics, smart optics, and rangefinders.

**Bottom:** Underbarrel and accessory equipment such as flashlights, lasers, foregrips, bipods, and secondary weapons. Side-mounted accessories normally use the Bottom slot as well.

**Muzzle:** Devices fitted to or modifying the muzzle, such as suppressors, compensators, muzzle brakes, and flash suppressors.

**Stock:** Stocks and rear assemblies such as folding, adjustable, precision, or recoil-reducing stocks.

A modification normally occupies one slot.

Installing a new modification in an occupied slot requires removing the existing modification first.

## Mod Compatibility

Having the correct modification slot does not automatically mean a mod fits every firearm.

Each modification lists the Frames it can be installed on and any additional requirements that apply.

Common requirements may include:

- Handgun or Full-Size Frame only.
- Automatic firearm required.
- Detachable magazine required.
- Compatible [[Ammunition and Explosives#Ammunition|ammunition]] feed required.
- Specific slot or weapon feature required.

Compatibility is handled by the individual modification rather than by adding more slot types or variable slot costs.

A firearm may therefore have an available slot but still be unable to accept a particular modification.

## Installing and Removing Mods

Each firearm modification lists its installation requirements and installation time.

**Simple Installation:** Mods designed to be fitted or removed without specialized work require no test. Spend the listed installation time.

**Armorer Installation:** Mods that alter the firearm's internal mechanisms, electronics, feed system, structure, or other complex components require an Armorer test. Spend the listed installation time, then make the test.

On a failed test, the installation fails and the time is spent.

A [[Complications|Complication]] may cause additional problems such as damaged components, lost parts, poor calibration, or damage to the firearm.

Removing a modification follows the same general procedure. Simple mods can normally be removed without a test, while integrated or complex modifications may require Armorer.

## Cosmetic Changes

Purely cosmetic alterations do not use modification slots.

Examples include paint, engraving, decals, decorative finishes, grip wrapping, and cosmetic lighting.

If a change provides a meaningful mechanical benefit, it is treated as a modification and must use an appropriate slot.
