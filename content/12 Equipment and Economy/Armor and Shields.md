Armor in 2092 runs from discreet protective clothing and reinforced streetwear to corporate security gear, tactical suits and military plate. Stopping bullets isn't everything: coverage, mobility, how well it hides, built-in systems and room for mods can matter just as much.

[[Cover and Shields#Shields|Shields]] are rarer, but riot units, security teams, breachers and specialists still carry them when holding an angle and surviving concentrated fire matter more than blending in.

## Armor Traits

- **[[Concealment]] X:** while worn, it's a hiding place with Concealment X. Any limits on what it can hide are in its entry.
- **Flexible:** it can be worn under other armor (see Layering).
- **Noisy:** −5 to Stealth while worn.
- **Restrictive:** −5 to tests that need a lot of physical movement while worn.

## Layering

Armor doesn't normally stack. If several pieces cover the same location, use the highest Armor. The exception is **Flexible** armor worn under something else: add its Armor to the outer layer's. Only one Flexible piece counts per location. The GM can rule that two pieces just can't be worn together.

## Fit

Armor has to fit the body wearing it. Ordinary differences in height and build are handled by normal fitting. Armor made for a very different body won't work until it's altered: different limb proportions, horns, a tail, unusual feet. The combat armor you took off a downed troll isn't going to fit a goblin. Altering it may take tools, materials, time and an Expertise test, depending on the armor. Only bother with this when the difference really matters.

Mods and Coatings are covered in [[Armor Modding]].
