A good crew carries more than guns. Medical gear, electronics, breaching tools, scanners, restraints, climbing gear and survival kit can matter as much as armor or ammunition when a job goes wrong.

## Tools and Kits

Some tasks need the right tools. Without them, the GM can apply a penalty, make the task take longer, limit what it can achieve, or rule it out entirely. Improvised or incomplete tools can still let you try, but they work worse than the real thing.

A **kit** holds the ordinary tools for one kind of work: electronics, mechanics, armorer, medical and so on. Some kits give extra benefits or hold limited supplies, as their entry says.

## Supplies

Some consumables, such as medical supplies, rations or chemical reagents, are easier to track loosely than one use at a time. They have a **Supply** value from 1 to 6. Ammunition, grenades and anything else tactical are always counted exactly.

Each type of Supply says when to make a **Supply Check**. When that happens, finish the action that triggered it, then roll as many d6s as the current Supply. **Each 1 lowers the Supply by 1.** At 0, it's used up.

Buying, scavenging, making or pooling compatible supplies raises the value again, up to 6. A piece of equipment can have its own Supply: once it's used up, the equipment still works, but can't do anything that needs those materials.
