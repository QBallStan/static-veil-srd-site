
Static Veil does not track injury through a pool of Hit Points. Instead, characters suffer Wounds of increasing severity.

After Armor, Armor Penetration, and other applicable effects are resolved, compare the remaining Damage to the Wound thresholds below.

| Final Damage | Wound Inflicted |
|---:|---|
| 1–2 | Grazing Wound |
| 3–4 | Minor Wound |
| 5–6 | Major Wound |
| 7+ | Critical Wound |

If the final Damage is 0 or less, the attack causes no Wound.

Characters have a limited number of Wound Slots at each severity. A typical protagonist has:

| Wound Type | Normal Slots |
|---|---:|
| Grazing | 3 |
| Minor | 2 |
| Major | 1 |
| Critical | 3 |

When a character suffers a Wound, mark an empty slot of that severity.

If no slot of the required severity remains, increase the Wound to the next severity and mark an available slot there instead. Continue increasing the severity until an available slot is found.

If a Wound would increase to Critical severity and the character has no available Critical Wound Slot, the character dies.

## Grazing Wounds

A **Grazing Wound** represents superficial harm such as bruises, shallow cuts, minor burns, glancing impacts, or pain that does not yet meaningfully impair the character.

Grazing Wounds impose no direct penalty.

## Minor Wounds

A **Minor Wound** represents a painful but manageable injury, such as a deeper cut, heavy bruise, strained joint, or impact that disrupts movement and focus.

Each Minor Wound imposes a −5 penalty to all tests.

## Major Wounds

A **Major Wound** represents serious injury, such as a broken bone, deep trauma, severe bleeding, torn muscle, or another injury that threatens the character's ability to keep fighting.

Each Major Wound imposes a −10 penalty to all tests.

## Critical Wounds

A **Critical Wound** represents devastating trauma and immediately removes the character from the fight.

When a protagonist suffers a Critical Wound, mark one available Critical Wound Slot. The character gains [[Death and Incapacitation|Incapacitated]] and cannot continue participating in combat. The associated [[Critical Injuries|Critical Injury]] may also impose additional immediate effects.

A typical protagonist has 3 Critical Wound Slots. If a protagonist suffers a Critical Wound while all of their Critical Wound Slots are already filled, they die unless a rule or the circumstances state otherwise.

Each Critical Wound also inflicts a Critical Injury, normally determined by the location struck. Psychic damage uses its own Critical Injury table instead of a [[Hit Locations and Damage#Hit Locations|Hit Location]].

## NPC Survival

When a minor NPC suffers a Critical Wound, they are normally removed from the fight and presumed dead or dying.

If their survival matters, roll 1d6 at the end of the scene for each minor NPC taken out by a Critical Wound. On a 6, the NPC survives.

A surviving NPC remains [[Death and Incapacitation|Incapacitated]] and unable to continue fighting until treated or otherwise recovered.

## Non-Lethal Attacks

A character may choose to attack with non-lethal intent when the weapon and circumstances make this reasonably possible. This must be declared before the attack roll.

Resolve the attack normally, but it cannot inflict a Critical Wound. If the attack would inflict a Critical Wound, the target instead becomes [[Conditions#Unconscious|Unconscious]].

Some attacks cannot reasonably be used non-lethally, including explosives, heavy ordnance, and other overwhelmingly destructive effects. The GM determines when non-lethal intent is possible.
