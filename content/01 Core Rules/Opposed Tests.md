
Use an **Opposed Test** when two characters directly compete against one another and the outcome depends on their relative performance.

Each participant makes an appropriate test. They do not need to use the same [[Attributes|Attributes]] or Skill, provided each combination reflects how that character is approaching the contest.

A failed test generates 0 [[Success Targets and Hits#Hits|Hits]] for the purpose of an Opposed Test. Negative Hits are ignored.

Resolve the contest as follows:

- If only one participant succeeds, that participant wins.
- If both participants succeed, compare their total Hits. The participant with more Hits wins.
- The difference between their Hits is the winner’s Net Hits.
- If both participants fail, neither side wins and the situation normally remains unchanged.
- If both participants generate the same number of Hits, the result is a tie. When one character is actively trying to overcome another’s resistance, a tie favors the resisting character.

Net Hits can be used when the strength of the victory matters. If the contest only requires a winner, no further interpretation is necessary.

## Complications

Resolve [[Complications]] after determining the winner.

A Complication does not change who wins the Opposed Test or alter the number of Hits generated. Instead, it introduces an additional setback appropriate to the character and situation.

A character may therefore win an Opposed Test while still suffering a Complication.

## Net Hits

**Net Hits** measure how much one result exceeds another.

To calculate Net Hits, subtract the lower Hit total from the higher Hit total:

**Net Hits = Higher Hits − Lower Hits**

Only positive Hits are used when calculating Net Hits. Failed tests and negative Hits count as 0 Hits.

Net Hits are used whenever the degree of advantage between two results matters, including Opposed Tests and combat.
