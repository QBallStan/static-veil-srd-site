
The **Success Target** is the number you need to roll equal to or under on a test.

Most tests add one [[Attributes|Attribute]] and one Skill:

**Success Target = Attribute + Skill**

Each Skill lists the Attribute it normally uses. If the character's approach clearly relies on a different one, the player can suggest it and the GM decides.

If no Skill fits, make an [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Attribute Check]] instead and add the two Attributes that best fit the action:

**Success Target = Attribute + Attribute**

If one Attribute clearly covers the whole action, add it to itself.

Apply all relevant [[Modifiers|modifiers]] before rolling. The final Success Target can't go below 5 or above 150.

A Success Target above 100 still fails on a roll of 96–00, but it generates more Hits when the roll succeeds.

## Hits

**Hits** measure how well a successful test went. You only need them when the degree of success matters.

To find the Hits, drop the last digit of both the roll and the Success Target, then subtract the roll from the target. A failed test generates 0 Hits.

For example, a roll of 34 against a Success Target of 65 becomes 3 against 6, for 3 Hits. The same roll against a Success Target of 42 becomes 3 against 4, for 1 Hit. A roll of 61 against 65 succeeds, but with 0 Hits. Against a Success Target of 118, a roll of 27 becomes 2 against 11, for 9 Hits.

## Outcomes

When the quality of a success matters, use the Hits to describe it.

| Hits | Outcome |
|---|---|
| 6 or more | Exceptional Success |
| 4 to 5 | Major Success |
| 2 to 3 | Strong Success |
| 0 to 1 | Narrow Success |

Some rules use the number of Hits directly instead of this table.
