
Roll only when the outcome is uncertain and both success and failure would change the situation. If the character can't fail, or failing wouldn't matter, don't roll.

To make a test:

1. Work out the [[Success Targets and Hits|Success Target]], usually the character's [[Attributes|Attribute]] plus their Skill.
2. Apply any [[Modifiers|modifiers]], including the difficulty set by the GM.
3. Roll d100, along with any [[Bonus Dice]].
4. If the d100 result is equal to or under the Success Target, the test succeeds. Otherwise, it fails. Bonus Dice can't change this.
5. If the quality of the success matters, work out the [[Success Targets and Hits#Hits|Hits]], including any from Bonus Dice.
6. If the roll triggers a [[Complications|Complication]], resolve it.

A roll of 01–05 always succeeds and a roll of 96–00 always fails, whatever the Success Target.

Most of the time, a success just means the character does what they set out to do, and you can skip step 5.
