**Karma** is a small pool of luck that player characters spend to turn a bad moment around.

Each player character starts with 3 Karma, which is also their normal maximum. Karma can never go above the maximum.

A character regains 1 Karma after a full night's rest, and 1 Karma whenever they suffer a [[Complications|Complication]]. The GM can also refill everyone's Karma at the start of a session, or award Karma when it fits.

## Spending Karma

Each option below costs 1 Karma. Unless a rule says otherwise, you can spend Karma whenever its effect applies.

### Run It Back

After making a test, reroll either the d100 or all the [[Bonus Dice]] from that test. You can only do this once per test, and the new result stands, even if it's worse.

If you reroll the d100, the new roll replaces the first one completely, including any Complication it triggered. If the new roll triggers a Complication, you can still spend another Karma on Not Today to ignore it.

### Shake It Off

Remove one stack of an ongoing negative [[Conditions|Condition]]. This can't remove [[Conditions#Unconscious|Unconscious]], Incapacitated, or any Condition that says it can't be removed this way.

### Jump the Line

At the start of a round, before anyone acts, move to the top of the [[Initiative and Action Points#Initiative|Initiative]] order for that round. Your normal Initiative doesn't change, and you go back to your usual place next round.

If several characters Jump the Line in the same round, they all act before everyone else, and the players decide the order among themselves.

### Push Through

Once per round, gain 1 extra Action Point for the current round. This AP can take you above your normal maximum, but you lose it if you don't spend it by the end of the round.

### Not Today

When you trigger a Complication, ignore it. You don't regain the Karma that Complication would have given you.

### One Last Shot

When you're about to become Incapacitated or die, take one final action straight away. You still need enough [[Initiative and Action Points#Action Points and Actions|Action Points]] to perform it.

### Lucky Break

Introduce a small, plausible detail or coincidence into the scene. The GM has the final say and can reject anything that contradicts established facts, gets around a major obstacle, or is bigger than a minor advantage.
