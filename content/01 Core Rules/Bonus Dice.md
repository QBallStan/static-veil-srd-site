
Some equipment, abilities, circumstances, and special rules grant **Bonus Dice**. Bonus Dice range from d4 to d12 and are rolled alongside the associated d100 test.

Bonus Dice do not change the [[Success Targets and Hits|Success Target]]. Instead, they can generate additional [[Success Targets and Hits#Hits|Hits]] when the d100 test succeeds.

Each Bonus Die generates Bonus Hits according to its result:

| Die Result | Bonus Hits |
|---|---:|
| 1–2 | 0 |
| 3–4 | 1 |
| 5–6 | 2 |
| 7–8 | 3 |
| 9–10 | 4 |
| 11–12 | 5 |

Add the Bonus Hits from all applicable Bonus Dice to the Hits generated by the d100.

If the d100 test fails, Bonus Dice normally generate no Hits. Some rules may allow Bonus Dice to function without a successful test.

Bonus Dice from separate sources stack unless a rule states otherwise or the sources cannot reasonably apply to the same action.
