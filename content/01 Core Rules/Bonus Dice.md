Some equipment, abilities and circumstances grant **Bonus Dice**: d6s rolled as a pool alongside a d100 test.

Each Bonus Die showing 4, 5 or 6 is a **Bonus Hit**. A 1, 2 or 3 does nothing. Bonus Hits are added to the Hits from the d100 roll.

Rules give Bonus Dice as a number of dice, such as **2d6**. When a rating grants Bonus Dice, you roll one die per point, so a rating of 3 gives 3d6.

Bonus Dice from different sources stack, unless a rule says otherwise or the sources couldn't reasonably apply to the same action.

## When Bonus Dice Count

Bonus Hits only count if the d100 test succeeds, and Bonus Dice can never turn a failed test into a success.

The exceptions are [[Active and Bonus Defense#Bonus Defense|Bonus Defense]], which always counts, and a few subsystems that explain their own dice where they use them.
