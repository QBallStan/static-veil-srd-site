A **Complication** is something that goes wrong on top of the result of a test.

Any d100 test that rolls 11, 33, 55, 77 or 99 triggers a Complication. Resolve the test normally first. The Complication doesn't change the roll, the [[Success Targets and Hits|Success Target]] or the [[Success Targets and Hits#Hits|Hits]].

When a player character suffers a Complication, they regain 1 [[Karma]], up to their maximum.

The GM decides what the Complication is, based on the action and the situation. Some rules define their own Complications.

**On a success**, the character still gets what they wanted, but it costs them. The job takes longer than planned, a guard notices, or a tool breaks halfway through.

**On a failure**, the action fails and things get worse. The character burns through resources, puts themselves in danger, or runs into a new obstacle.

## Trying Again

A failed test usually can't be repeated unless something changes. The character might find new information, switch tools, get help or try a different approach.

Some tasks allow repeated attempts because each try costs time or resources, or brings its own risk. The rules for those tasks say when another test is allowed.

If nothing has changed and failing again wouldn't cost anything, don't roll again.
