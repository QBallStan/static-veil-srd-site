# Playtest General Equipment Catalogue

> [!warning] Playtest Draft
> This is a working equipment catalogue, not final SRD content. Prices, availability, manufacturers, and item modifications remain to be added.

## Equipment Conventions

- **Skill** identifies the test the equipment normally supports. An em dash means no test is normally required.
- Ordinary professional equipment usually **enables the test without missing-tools penalties**. It does not grant a Bonus Die.
- Listed Bonus Dice apply only to the stated Skill and are normally [[Bonus Dice#Hot Bonus Dice|Hot]].
- **Supplies** simply identifies whether an item consumes meaningful materials. When it does, use the normal [[Tools and General Equipment#Supplies|Supply]] rules. Batteries and ordinary recharging do not use Supplies.
- Bulk measures how hard an item is to conceal, not its weight.
