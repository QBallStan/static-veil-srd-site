
A character who suffers a [[Wounds#Critical Wounds|Critical Wound]] is normally **Incapacitated**, not killed. They mark a Critical Wound Slot, resolve the [[Critical Injuries|Critical Injury]] that comes with it, and are out of the fight.

## Incapacitated

An Incapacitated character is conscious unless their injury says otherwise. They can talk and take Free Actions, but they can't spend AP, move under their own power or make Active Defenses, and they count as [[Defenseless Targets|Defenseless]]. Being Incapacitated doesn't mean they're dying. Unless their Critical Injury is [[Critical Injuries#Unstable|Unstable]], they stay alive until something else threatens their life.

## Death

A character dies when:

- they suffer a Critical Wound with all their Critical Wound Slots already filled,
- an Unstable injury's timer runs out before they get the treatment they need,
- an effect says it kills them, or
- surviving is clearly impossible.

Otherwise, becoming Incapacitated never kills a character by itself.
