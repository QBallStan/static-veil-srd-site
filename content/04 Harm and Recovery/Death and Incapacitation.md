
A character who suffers a [[Wounds#Critical Wounds|Critical Wound]] is normally **Incapacitated** rather than killed outright. They mark a [[Wounds|Critical Wound]] Slot, resolve the associated [[Critical Injuries|Critical Injury]], and are removed from the fight.

Being Incapacitated does not mean the character is dying. Unless the Critical Injury is [[Critical Injuries#Unstable|Unstable]], the character remains alive until another rule or circumstance threatens their life.

A character dies when any of the following occurs:

- They suffer a Critical Wound while all of their Critical Wound Slots are filled.
- An Unstable injury's death timer expires before the character receives the required treatment.
- An effect specifically states that it causes death.
- Survival is clearly impossible given the circumstances.

Death is otherwise never an automatic consequence of becoming Incapacitated.
