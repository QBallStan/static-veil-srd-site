
Characters recover lost [[Wounds|Health]] through rest, medical care and other effects. Rest and ordinary care don't remove [[Critical Injuries]]. Those heal on their own timeline (see [[Critical Injuries#Critical Injury Recovery|Critical Injury Recovery]]).

## Natural Recovery

After **7 hours of uninterrupted rest**, a character makes a [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Vigor check]]. Each [[Success Targets and Hits#Hits|Hit]] restores **1 Health**. Only one check per rest. A rest that's seriously interrupted restores nothing.

## Field Treatment

Quick care right after an injury. It takes 1d6 minutes. Make a First Aid test. Each Hit restores 1 Health. Field Treatment only restores Health lost in the **past hour**, never more than was lost, and each character can only receive it once per fight.

Without proper tools and supplies, the test takes −10, and the GM can rule treatment impossible. On a failure, no Health is restored. A [[Complications|Complication]] might use up supplies, drag the treatment out or make an injury worse. Field Treatment can't remove a Critical Wound or shorten a Critical Injury's recovery.

## Assisted Recovery

A character who looks after a patient through a rest can help them heal. The caregiver makes a Medicine test, and their Hits are added to the patient's Hits on the recovery check. This needs proper medical supplies, regular access to the patient and conditions that allow ongoing care. Only one caregiver can help each patient per rest, but a caregiver can look after several patients if time and supplies allow.

## Stabilization

Stabilization stops an [[Critical Injuries#Unstable|Unstable]] Critical Injury from killing the character. It costs 3 AP and needs physical access to the patient. Make a First Aid test, at −10 without proper medical supplies. The GM can rule it impossible when the necessary care can't reasonably be given.

On a success, one Unstable injury is stabilized and its death timer stops. Remove 1 stack of [[Conditions#Bleeding|Bleeding]], plus 1 more for each Hit. On a failure, the timer keeps running. Each Unstable injury has to be stabilized separately.

If the injury needs Advanced or Extraordinary Treatment to survive, stabilizing replaces its timer with **1d6 hours**. Stabilizing it again can't extend that deadline.

## Treating Critical Injuries

An injury marked [[Critical Injuries#Treatment Required|Treatment Required]] needs a Medicine test with proper tools and supplies. It takes 1d6 × 10 minutes, more or less for very simple or difficult procedures. On a success, the injury can start recovering. A failure doesn't make it worse by itself, but trying again takes more time. A Complication might use up supplies or aggravate the injury. Once treated, the injury doesn't need treating again unless something undoes it, such as a removed splint, a reopened wound or a new injury to the same spot.

## Advanced Treatment

Surgery or another [[Critical Injuries#Advanced Treatment|specialized procedure]]. It needs proper medical equipment and a suitable place to work. Make a Medicine test. It takes 1d6 hours, adjusted for the injury and facilities. On a success, it prevents any consequence the injury says Advanced Treatment can stop. On a failure, it can be tried again if there's time and the resources are still there. A Complication might use up supplies, drag the procedure out or make the patient worse.

## Extraordinary Treatment

[[Critical Injuries#Extraordinary Treatment|Medicine beyond ordinary surgery]], such as organ replacement or neural reconstruction. It needs specialized facilities and equipment, and a test with the right medical Expertise. It takes 1d6 × 4 hours, or longer for very extensive work. On a success, it prevents or restores any consequence the injury says Extraordinary Treatment can address. On a failure, it can be tried again if the patient survives and the time and resources are still there. A Complication might use up rare resources, drag the procedure out or make the patient worse.
