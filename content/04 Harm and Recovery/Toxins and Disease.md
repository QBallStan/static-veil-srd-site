Poisons and diseases work the same way. A substance or an infection gets into the body, takes effect after a while, lasts for a while, and the body either resists it or recovers. [[Drugs and Addiction|Drugs]] use the same profile, but have their own rules.

## Substances

Every substance has a short profile:
- **Vector:** inhaled, ingested, injected or contact.
- **Potency:** how hard it is to resist.
- **Onset:** how long it takes to act, from instantly to several hours.
- **Effect** and **Duration**.

**Potency** is a difficulty. The [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Vigor]] check to resist takes its modifier:

| Potency | Modifier | Examples |
|---|---:|---|
| Mild | +0 | Cheap sedative, irritant gas |
| Strong | −20 | Military tranquilizer, most venoms |
| Deadly | −40 | Nerve agents, designer poisons |

A success resists the substance, and a failure means you suffer its effect. Hits only matter if the substance says so.

## Toxins

When exposed, make a Vigor check at the toxin's Potency once it takes effect. On a failure, you suffer its effect: Fatigue, a penalty, Stunned, Unconscious, or damage that ignores Armor and Grit.

A **lethal** toxin keeps working. It calls for a new check at each interval it lists, until one succeeds or until someone stops it, with an antidote or a successful Medicine test.

> **Neurotoxin dart:** Injected · Strong · Onset 1 round · 2 damage per round, ignoring Armor and Grit · Lethal, every round.

## Diseases

A disease also has an **Incubation** time and a **Severity** from 1 to 3. Once incubation is over, the patient makes a Vigor check at its Potency every day. On a failure, they suffer its symptoms for that day, usually Fatigue or a penalty. They recover once they've had as many successes as its Severity, whether in a row or not.

A caregiver who succeeds on a Medicine test gives the patient +20 on that day's check. Some diseases need [[Healing and Medical Care#Advanced Treatment|Advanced Treatment]] to be cured.
