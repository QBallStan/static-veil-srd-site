
Conditions are temporary states that affect a character on top of [[Wounds|Health]] penalties. They come from attacks, injuries, hazards, magic, equipment and other effects.

Some Conditions use stacks. When a Condition is applied again, add the new stacks to the existing ones, up to its maximum. Unless it says otherwise, the effects of each stack add up. A Condition lasts until its duration runs out, its removal requirement is met, or something removes it. Most can be removed with [[Karma#Shake It Off|Shake It Off]]. Those that can't say so.

## Bleeding

**Max Stacks:** 3 · **Duration:** Until removed

Blood loss from open wounds, internal trauma or hemorrhaging. At the start of your first turn each round, take 1 damage per stack. [[Sub-Attributes#Grit|Grit]] doesn't reduce it. Bleeding doesn't get worse on its own. [[Healing and Medical Care#Stabilization|Stabilization]] removes 1 stack, plus 1 for each Hit, and other treatments or effects can remove it as their rules say.

## Burning

**Max Stacks:** 10 · **Duration:** Until put out

You're on fire. At the start of your first turn each round, take 2 damage per stack, with ArP −2 per stack. It hits the Torso for Armor and [[Hit Locations and Damage#Hit Locations|Hit Location]], and Grit doesn't reduce it. Then gain 1 more stack, up to the maximum.

To put the flames out, spend 2 AP and make an Agility + Resolve [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Attribute Check]]. Each [[Success Targets and Hits#Hits|Hit]] removes 1 stack. Diving into water, a fire extinguisher, magic or another method can remove more stacks, or put the fire out completely.

## Chilled

**Max Stacks:** 4 · **Duration:** Remove 1 stack at the start of your first turn each round

Numbness, stiffness and loss of coordination from extreme cold. Each stack gives −1 [[Sub-Attributes#Movement|Movement]] and −5 to all tests. If the cold keeps coming, from a freezing environment or a sustained cryogenic effect, stacks can keep building as that source says. Heat, shelter, protective gear or magic can remove extra stacks at the GM's discretion.

## Corroding

**Duration:** Until removed

Acid or another corrosive eating through flesh and armor. At the end of your first turn each round, resolve all your Corroding stacks as one instance of damage: 2 damage and ArP −2 per stack. There's no defense against it, and Grit doesn't reduce it. Then remove 1 stack.

If Corroding's ArP ever brings the Armor on that location to 0, mark that Armor as Corroded. Once the stacks from that exposure are gone, the Armor on a Corroded location is reduced by 1 until it's repaired. Proper cleaning, neutralizing or other suitable treatment removes all remaining stacks.

## Distracted

**Max Stacks:** 4 · **Duration:** Until removed

Confusion, sensory overload, suppressing fire or anything else that breaks focus. Each stack gives −5 to all tests. On your turn, you can spend 1 AP to **Refocus** and remove all stacks.

## Stunned

**Max Stacks:** 3 · **Duration:** Until removed

Shock, disorientation or a blow that rattles the nervous system. Each stack costs you 1 AP, down to a minimum of 1 AP. While Stunned, your Movement is halved, however many stacks you have. At the start of your first turn each round, make a [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Vigor check]]. Each Hit removes 1 stack. Once they're all gone, you act normally with whatever AP you have left.

## Prone

**Max Stacks:** 1 · **Duration:** Until you stand up

Knocked down, or deliberately fighting from the ground. While Prone:

- Melee attacks against you get +10. Against ranged attacks, you count as being in [[Cover and Shields#Prone|Light Cover]], unless the attacker is within 2 meters.
- Your own melee attacks take −10, unless your target is also Prone.
- You can crawl at half Movement.

Standing up costs 1 AP. Dropping Prone on purpose is a Free Action on your turn.

## Unconscious

**Max Stacks:** 1 · **Duration:** Set by the cause

You're unaware of your surroundings and can't act. You can't take actions, spend AP, move on purpose or make Active Defenses, and you count as [[Defenseless Targets|Defenseless]]. Unless the cause says otherwise, you fall Prone and drop whatever you're holding. Injuries, drugs, toxins, medical procedures and magic can each set their own duration or way to wake up. Shake It Off can't remove Unconscious.

## Restrained

**Max Stacks:** 4

Pinned, tied up, trapped or tangled. Your Movement is 0, and you take −5 per stack to tests that need real physical movement. Anything that doesn't is unaffected. Escaping usually costs 2 AP and a test that fits the restraint, and the Restrained penalty applies if the escape relies on movement. Whatever caused the restraint decides how it can be escaped, broken, hacked or dispelled.

## Blinded

**Max Stacks:** 5 · **Duration:** Remove 1 stack at the start of your first turn each round

Your vision is impaired by injury, bright light, smoke, chemicals or darkness. Each stack gives −5 to tests that rely on sight. At 5 stacks, you can't see, and anything that needs sight fails automatically unless another sense, ability or piece of equipment lets you manage.

## Deafened

**Max Stacks:** 5 · **Duration:** Remove 1 stack at the start of your first turn each round

Your hearing is impaired by noise, pressure, injury or something blocking your ears. Each stack gives −5 to tests that rely on hearing. At 5 stacks, you can't hear, and you miss speech, warnings, nearby movement and anything else carried only by sound, unless another sense, ability or device makes up for it.

## Frightened

**Max Stacks:** 3 · **Duration:** Until overcome

Overwhelming fear of a specific creature, object, place or event. Each stack gives −5 to all tests.

- At 1 stack, you can't willingly move closer to the source.
- At 2 stacks, any movement you choose has to take you farther away.
- At 3 stacks, you have to use your Movement to get away from it as directly as you can.

Fear never forces you into an obviously worse or more immediate danger. At the start of your first turn each round, make a [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Willpower check]]. Each Hit removes 1 stack.

## Fatigue

**Max Stacks:** 4

Each stack gives −5 to all tests. At 4 stacks, you're close to collapse. If you'd gain another stack, make a Vigor check. On a failure, you become [[Death and Incapacitation|Incapacitated]]. How Fatigue is removed depends on its source (see [[Endurance]]). Drugs, magic, augmentation or medical treatment can work differently. Shake It Off can't remove Fatigue.
