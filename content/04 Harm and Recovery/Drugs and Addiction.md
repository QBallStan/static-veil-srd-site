Stims to stay awake, combat drugs to move faster, painkillers to keep going with a bullet in your leg. In 2092, chemistry is as much a part of the kit as chrome, and it sends its bill later.

## Taking a Drug

A drug you take **willingly** works, with no roll. Taking a dose is a Minor Action (1 AP), unless the drug says otherwise. Its profile gives:

- **Vector:** inhaled, ingested, injected or a patch.
- **Onset:** usually immediate.
- **Effect** and **Duration:** the benefit, and how long it lasts.
- **Crash:** the cost when it wears off.
- **Addiction:** from 0 to 3.
- **Craving:** how long a dependent user can go between doses. If it isn't listed, it's 24 hours.

**Drugged against your will:** someone drugged without consenting, through a spiked drink or a forced injection, resists with the drug's **Potency**, like a [[Toxins and Disease|toxin]].

## Crash

When the effect wears off, the character suffers its Crash, usually [[Conditions#Fatigue|Fatigue]], sometimes a penalty or a Condition. The Crash can't be avoided, and it doesn't wait for a good moment.

## Doubling Up

A second dose of the same drug while it's still active **doesn't add to the effect**. It extends the Duration, but **both Crashes add up**, and each dose calls for its own Addiction check.

## Addiction and Dependence

After each dose of a drug with Addiction X, make a [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Willpower]] check at **−10 × X**. On a failure, gain 1 stack of **Dependence** on that drug, up to 3. An Addiction 0 drug never causes Dependence.

**Withdrawal:** a dependent character who goes longer than the drug's Craving without a dose takes **−5 per stack** to all tests, until they take one or quit. Taking a dose ends the penalty, but calls for a new Addiction check.

**Quitting:** at the end of each week without a dose, make a Willpower check. Each success removes 1 stack, and the withdrawal penalty applies the whole time. Clinical treatment (a detox program, medical supervision) gives +20 to that check. [[Karma#Shake It Off|Shake It Off]] can't remove Dependence.

## Example

> **Jolt:** Injected · Immediate · Duration 1 scene · +1 AP per round, ignore Fatigue penalties · Crash: 2 Fatigue · Addiction 2 · Craving 24 hours.
