[[Hacking#Scripts|Scripts]] are grouped by function to make them easier to find. The groups don't change how they work.

Each Script lists whether it's **Passive** or **Active**, its **[[Hacking#Scripts|RAM]]** cost, whether it works as a [[Hacking#Covert Actions and Attacks|covert action or an attack]], and its **Effect**.
