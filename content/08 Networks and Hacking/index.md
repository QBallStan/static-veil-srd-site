The [[The Grid|Grid]] connects nearly everything: phones, IDs, guns, cars, cameras, doors, traffic lights, the AR overlay most people see the city through. Most of the time nobody thinks about it. It's just there, like the air.

It isn't a separate virtual world you log into. It's a layer of infrastructure running through the physical one, and every lock, turret and drone answers to a [[The Grid#Hosts|Host]] somewhere. A [[Hacking|GridRunner]] breaks into those Hosts with a [[Interfaces#Decks and Cold Cradles|Deck]] and makes them do what they want: open the vault door, wipe the footage, turn the security drones on their owners. The trick is finishing before the Host notices. Every hard push makes [[Hacking#Noise|Noise]], and nobody knows exactly how much it takes before the system starts fighting back.

Some GridRunners work through AR, with one eye on the fight around them. Others plug in and go [[Interfaces#Deep Interface|Deep]], where everything comes easier and faster, and where the Host's [[Hacking#IC|IC]] can reach back down the link and fry their nervous system.

Hacking happens alongside everything else. A GridRunner uses the same rounds, AP and tests as the rest of the crew. They just aim them at the building instead of the guards.
