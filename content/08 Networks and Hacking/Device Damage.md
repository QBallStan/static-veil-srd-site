Devices don't have a health track. When something goes wrong with one, whether it's hostile code, a power surge or a bullet, it's in one of three states:

| State | Effect | How it ends |
|---|---|---|
| **Glitched** | It still works, but tests that rely on it take −10. | A reboot, or the end of the scene |
| **Disabled** | It doesn't work. | A reboot |
| **Bricked** | It's broken. | A repair |

A worse state replaces a milder one. A Glitched device that's Disabled is just Disabled.

The Glitched penalty only applies to tests that really depend on the device: a Glitched smart weapon penalizes attacks made with it, and a Glitched cybereye penalizes tests that use its sight.

Cyberware fails safe where it can. A Disabled or Bricked cybereye loses its enhancements but doesn't blind its user, and a cyberlimb loses its augmentation bonuses without becoming dead weight. Its user can still move it, just no better than flesh and bone.

## Causing Device States

- **Hacking:** a GridRunner causes these states with a [[Hacking#Attacks|Disrupt]], or covertly with Control at the right authority: Glitched at User, Disabled at Admin, Bricked at Owner.
- **Physical damage:** shooting or smashing a device uses the normal [[Objects, Sneak Attacks, and Surprise#Attacking an Object|object rules]]. A device destroyed that way is Bricked.

> EMP effects are still to be written.

## Rebooting

**Reboot** is a Major Action by the device's owner, or anyone with authority over it. It clears Glitched and Disabled, and ends any [[Hacking#Attacks|Hijack]]. A rebooted device works normally from the start of its owner's next turn.

## Repairing

A Bricked device needs an **Electronics** test and about an hour with the right tools. On a success, it works again. On a failure, the device can't be saved without parts or specialist help, at the GM's discretion. Cyberware that's Bricked needs the same test, but it's repaired inside its user, so it usually needs a clinic, or at least a clean room and a steady hand.
