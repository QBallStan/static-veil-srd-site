Vehicles are everywhere in Static Veil: commuter cars and cargo haulers, armored transports, courier bikes, speedboats, VTOLs, the odd industrial walker. Most of the time they just get people where they're going, and nobody needs to roll for it.

It's when things go wrong that the rules matter: a turn taken too fast on wet asphalt, a VTOL limping home with a shot-out rotor, or a corporate sedan that won't stop following you. [[Operating Vehicles]] covers what the driver or pilot rolls, and what happens when they lose control. [[Chases and Vehicle Combat]] covers the rest: closing the gap or opening it, weaving through traffic, ramming, dogfights, and everyone else in the vehicle leaning out of the windows with a gun.
