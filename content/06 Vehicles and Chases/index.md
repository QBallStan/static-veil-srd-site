Vehicles are a constant part of life in Static Veil, from commuter cars and cargo haulers to armored transports, motorcycles, watercraft, VTOLs, and heavy industrial walkers. They carry people and equipment through dense cities, corporate enclaves, wastelands, and other dangerous environments.

Most travel requires no special rules. [[Operating Vehicles]] covers difficult operation, vehicle Tests, Handling, Speed Bands, crews, and loss of control. [[Chases and Vehicle Combat]] covers pursuit, dogfighting, hazards, boarding, ramming, and combat involving moving vehicles.
