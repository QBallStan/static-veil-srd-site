
**Summoning** is the magical discipline of contacting, manifesting, commanding, negotiating with, binding, and banishing [[Spirits]].

Summoning is separate from [[Spellcasting]] and [[Ritual Magic]]. Access to particular spirits and [[Spirit Aspects]] is determined by the practitioner's [[Traditions|Tradition]].

Different Traditions may understand the entities involved in completely different ways. The rules use **Spirits** as a general term regardless of how a particular Tradition describes them.

> [!NOTE]  
> Summoning rules are still in development. The summoning procedure, spirit interactions, binding, banishing, and Spirit catalogue will be added once the system is finalized.
