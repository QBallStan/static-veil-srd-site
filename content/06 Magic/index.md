Magic is the ability to manifest supernatural effects through a connection to the [[Veil]]. This connection is represented by **[[Anima]]**, which measures how strongly a character remains connected to their soul.

Magically active characters generally express their abilities in one of two ways.

**Spellcasters** use their Anima to manifest effects beyond themselves. They may cast [[Spells]], perform [[Ritual Magic]], interact with [[Spirits]] through [[Summoning]], or use other practices allowed by their [[Traditions|Tradition]].

**Mystics** turn their magical potential inward. Instead of casting spells, they develop [[Mystic Powers]] that enhance or transform their own capabilities.

Most magically active characters are either Spellcasters or Mystics. Rare individuals capable of both are known as **[[Hybrids]]**.

Magic does not use a pool of expendable mana. Casting instead creates **[[Strain]]**, temporarily stressing the connection between the caster and their soul. More powerful manifestations create more Strain, and repeatedly casting beyond what the caster can safely tolerate can cause [[Fatigue]] or [[Wounds]].

Different [[Traditions]] explain magic, the Veil, spirits, and the soul in very different ways. The rules describe how these phenomena function mechanically without requiring every tradition to agree on why they function.
