---
title: Magic
---

Magic is the ability to manifest supernatural effects through **[[Attributes#Anima|Anima]]**, a metaphysical Attribute associated with the self, the soul, and interaction with the Veil.

Magically active characters generally express their abilities in one of two ways.

**Spellcasters** use Anima to manifest effects beyond themselves. They cast [[Spellcasting|spells]], perform [[Ritual Magic]], interact with **Spirits** through **Summoning**, or use other practices allowed by their [[Traditions|Tradition]].

**Mystics** turn their magical potential inward. Instead of casting spells, they develop **Mystic Powers** that enhance or transform their own capabilities.

Most magically active characters are either Spellcasters or Mystics. Rare individuals capable of both are known as **Hybrids**.

Magic does not use a pool of expendable mana. Spellcasting instead creates **[[Spellcasting#Strain|Strain]]**, temporarily stressing the caster's Anima. More powerful manifestations can create more Strain, and repeatedly casting beyond what the caster can safely tolerate can cause [[Conditions#Fatigue|Fatigue]] or [[Wounds]].

Different [[Traditions]] explain magic, the Veil, spirits, souls, and other supernatural phenomena in different ways. These explanations are matters of belief, culture, and practice unless the rules explicitly state otherwise.
