# Playtest Weapon Catalogue

> [!warning] Playtest Draft
> This is a balancing worksheet, not the final equipment catalogue. It deliberately excludes prices, availability, manufacturers, accessories, ammunition variants, explosives, and area-effect weapons.

## Catalogue Conventions

- **Damage** is listed as **flat Damage + a pool of Bonus Dice** (1d6 to 5d6).
- **Unarmed** remains a baseline rather than a catalogue weapon: **0 + Damage Modifier**, with no weapon die.
- **ArP** (Armor Penetration) reduces Armor. ArP ranges from **0** to **−10**.
- A category anchor may vary by **−2 to +2** for a particular model. Flat Damage can never exceed **16**.
- **Range** is in meters and intentionally compressed for playability. All listed ranges are multiples of 4.
- Melee attacks add the wielder's [[Sub-Attributes#Damage Modifier|Damage Modifier]]. Thrown attacks do not.
- An attack that lands then has to get through the target's [[Active and Bonus Defense#Damage Reduction|Damage Reduction]]: effective Armor and Grit.
- Capacity notation follows [[Ammunition and Explosives#Capacity|the SRD ammunition codes]].
- Parry dice are a separate defensive Trait, not part of the weapon's damage dice.
- **Minimum Strength X** is a [[Weapons#Weapon Traits|Weapon Trait]], given only to weapons that really need the strength. The highest requirement is 5: a maxed-out, augmented human or a stronger Lineage.
