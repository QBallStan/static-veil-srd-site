
**Mystics** are magically active characters whose magic is directed inward rather than manifested as [[Spellcasting|spells]].

Mystics develop **[[Mystics#Mystic Powers|Mystic Powers]]** that enhance or alter their own capabilities. Some powers are permanently active, while others must be activated when needed.

Mystic Powers are purchased with **Power Points** derived from [[Anima]]. Power Points represent the amount of magical potential a Mystic can invest in their powers. They are not spent during play and do not need to be recovered.

Unlike [[Spellcasting]], Mystic Powers do not normally use [[Spellcasting#Intensity|Intensity]] or generate [[Spellcasting#Strain|Strain]].

Most magically active characters are either Spellcasters or Mystics. Rare individuals are capable of both forms of magic. These characters are known as **Hybrid Mystics**.
## Mystic Powers

**Mystic Powers** are supernatural abilities developed by [[Mystics]] through their [[Anima]].

Mystic Powers only affect the Mystic themselves or abilities that originate from their own body. They do not function as [[Spellcasting|spells]] and cannot normally be used to directly place magical effects on other creatures, objects, or areas.

Mystic Powers are purchased with **Power Points**. Each power has a listed Power Point cost.

If a Mystic Power requires an action, activation cost, duration, test, or other condition, its entry in the [[Mystics#Mystic Power Catalogue|Mystic Power Catalogue]] specifies those rules.

Mystic Powers do not normally use [[Spellcasting#Intensity|Intensity]], require [[Spellcasting#Concentration|Concentration]], or generate [[Spellcasting#Strain|Strain]].

## Power Points

A Mystic has a number of **Power Points equal to their [[Anima]]**.

Power Points are used to acquire [[Mystics#Mystic Powers|Mystic Powers]]. Each power has a listed Power Point cost, and the total cost of a Mystic's powers cannot exceed their available Power Points.

Power Points are not spent when a power is used. They represent the amount of magical potential the Mystic has invested in their abilities.

When a Mystic's Anima increases, their available Power Points increase by the same amount. These additional points may be used to learn new Mystic Powers during play.
