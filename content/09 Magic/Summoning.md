**Summoning** is calling up, commanding, bargaining with, binding and banishing **spirits**. What a spirit actually is, nobody can say for sure. All anyone knows is that something answers when called. Each [[Traditions|Tradition]] has its own name for them and its own explanation. The rules just call them spirits.

Any practitioner can summon. Summoning tests use **Anima + Summoning**, with your [[Traditions#Affinities|Affinity]]'s modifier.

## Spirit Types

Every summoner can call **Guardian** and **Servant** spirits, and chooses two more Spirit Types. Their Tradition decides what they call these spirits and how they deal with them, but it doesn't limit which Types they can choose.
