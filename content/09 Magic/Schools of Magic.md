Spells are grouped into five **Schools** by what they actually do, not by how they look or what they lead to. A fireball is Destruction because it burns people. A spell that sets the curtains on fire to create a distraction is Alteration. The Schools are a classification for the rules: a Tradition can sort magic however it likes.

> [!warning] Work in progress
> The spell catalogues for every School are still to be written.

## Destruction

**Destruction** spells exist to do damage: bolts, blasts, area attacks, strikes, psychic assaults. They use the normal [[The Combat Exchange|combat rules]], and each spell gives its range, damage, [[Damage Types and Effects|damage delivery]] and anything else the attack needs.

Most use **Anima + Spellcasting**. A Destruction spell delivered by touch or through a weapon uses **Anima + Melee** and follows the [[Melee Combat]] rules, still with Intensity and Strain as normal.

### Destruction Aspects

An **Aspect** is the form a Destruction spell takes. Every caster has the **Telekinetic** Aspect and chooses two more. You can't use an Aspect you haven't chosen.

| Aspect | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Telekinetic** | +2 damage. Raw force, impact and pressure. |
| **Fire** | [[Damage Types and Effects#Fire\|Fire]] damage, with the normal Fire effects. |
| **Electric** | [[Damage Types and Effects#Electric\|Electric]] damage, with the normal Electric effects. |
| **Cold** | [[Damage Types and Effects#Cold\|Cold]] damage, with the normal Cold effects. |
| **Acid** | [[Damage Types and Effects#Acid\|Acid]] damage, with the normal Acid effects. |
| **Necrotic** | −2 damage. The damage is [[Damage Types and Effects#Necrotic\|Necrotic]]. |
| **Psychic** | −2 damage. The damage is [[Damage Types and Effects#Direct Damage\|Direct Damage]]. |

A Destruction spell is learned with one Aspect. The same spell with a different Aspect is a separate spell: knowing a fire bolt doesn't mean you know a cold one.

## Body

**Body** spells work on living bodies: they heal injuries, cure disease, neutralize toxins, strengthen, weaken or shut down biological functions.

A willing target doesn't resist. An unwilling one resists with a [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Vigor]] check, [[Opposed Tests|opposed]] to the casting test, unless the spell says otherwise.

## Perception

**Perception** spells grant senses beyond the ordinary, usually to the caster. Some can be given to a willing ally.

They're unopposed, unless they're trying to detect something actively hidden by Stealth, magic or anything else. In that case, the spell goes up against whatever is hiding it.

## Illusion

**Illusion** spells create false sensations. They have no physical presence and can't push, lift or hurt anything.

**Mental Illusions** play directly on the minds of one or more creatures. A willing target doesn't resist. An unwilling one resists with a [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Willpower]] check, opposed to the casting test.

**Projected Illusions** exist in the world. Anything that can perceive the right sense can perceive them, including cameras, microphones and sensors. There's no resistance test: the spell's Hits are how convincing the illusion is. Someone with a reason to doubt it, who looks closely or touches something that doesn't add up, can make an [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Insight]] check, and needs more Hits than the spell to see through it. That doesn't end the illusion, and it doesn't reveal it to anyone else.

## Alteration

**Alteration** spells control, move, reshape or transform creatures, objects, forces and places. Each one is Physical, Mental or Field.

- **Physical:** moving, shaping or transforming bodies, objects and forces. An unwilling creature resists with a [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Vigor]] check, opposed to the casting test. An object held by someone else is opposed by their [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Grip]]. Unattended objects don't resist, but a spell can require a minimum number of Hits depending on mass and construction.
- **Mental:** influencing thoughts, impulses and decisions. An unwilling target resists with a [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Willpower]] check, opposed to the casting test. A spell that lasts may give its target further chances to break free, as described in the spell.
- **Field:** changing an area rather than a target, such as rough ground, fog, interference, a barrier. It's unopposed when cast, and the spell says what it takes to get through it.
