**Vessels** are Awakened whose magic never leaves their body. They don't cast spells. Their Anima settles into flesh, nerves and reflexes, and changes what they can do. Clinics call them *somatics*.

Many Vessels feel drawn to something: the fight, the crowd, the craft, the hunt. They call it their **Calling**. A Calling has no rules attached. It's a way of saying what your powers are for, and it can shape which powers a Vessel learns.

## Vessel Powers

**Vessel Powers** only affect the Vessel, or things that come from their own body. They aren't spells, and they can't put magical effects on other people, objects or areas. Some are always on. Others have to be activated, and the power says how: its action, duration, any test and its limits.

Vessel Powers don't use Intensity, don't cause Strain and don't need Concentration, unless a power says otherwise.

## Power Points

A Vessel has **Power Points** equal to their [[Anima]] Rating, which is 1 to 4 for most of them. Each power costs a number of Power Points, and the total can't be higher than what the Vessel has. Power Points are never spent: they measure how much the Vessel has invested in their powers.

When their Anima Rating goes up, they get more Power Points and can learn new powers. When it goes down, they lose powers (see [[Anima#Losing Anima|Losing Anima]]).

## Mystics

A **Mystic** is both a Vessel and a Spellcaster. They get their full Power Points and can cast, and they choose an [[Traditions#Affinities|Affinity]] like any Spellcaster. Being both costs more at character creation, and a Mystic has fewer spells available than a pure Spellcaster.
