**Ritual Magic** is magic worked slowly, through preparation and procedure. A ritual can do things a spell can't, but it takes time, materials and care.

Any practitioner can perform rituals. Ritual tests use **Anima + Ritual Magic**, with your [[Traditions#Affinities|Affinity]]'s modifier.

> The procedure, requirements, costs and ritual catalogue are still to be written.
