
**Ritual Magic** is magic performed through preparation, time, and deliberate procedure rather than immediate [[Spellcasting]].

Rituals can achieve effects that are impractical or impossible to perform as ordinary spells, but require substantially more time and preparation.

Access to Ritual Magic depends on the practitioner's [[Traditions|Tradition]].

> [!NOTE]  
> Ritual Magic is still in development. Its procedures, requirements, costs, and ritual catalogue will be added once the system is finalized.
