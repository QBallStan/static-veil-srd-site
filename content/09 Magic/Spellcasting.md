**Spellcasting** shapes magic on the spot. Unlike [[Ritual Magic]], it's fast enough to use in a fight.

## Casting a Spell

Casting a spell is a Major Action unless the spell says otherwise. Choose the spell and its targets, then its **Intensity**, and roll **Anima + Spellcasting**, with your [[Traditions#Affinities|Affinity]]'s modifier, plus one [[Bonus Dice|Bonus Die]] per point of Intensity.

On a success, the Intensity dice add their Hits to the spell, and the spell takes effect. Each spell says what its Hits do: more damage, more targets, a longer duration, more information, a stronger effect. On a failure, nothing happens.

Either way, the Intensity dice cause [[#Strain|Strain]].

Some spells use a different test. [[Schools of Magic#Destruction|Destruction spells delivered by touch]], for example, use Anima + Melee.

## Intensity

**Intensity** is how hard the caster reaches through their [[Anima]]. It goes from **1 to 6**: you can't cast at Intensity 0. Higher Intensity means more Bonus Hits and a stronger spell, and more Strain. It doesn't make the spell any easier to cast.

**Overreaching:** if you cast at an Intensity higher than your Anima Rating, you can't resist the Strain. You take all of it.

## Strain

**Strain** is the backlash of pushing magic through yourself. Every Hit on the Intensity dice is 1 potential Strain, whether the spell worked or not. If the dice roll no Hits, there's no Strain.

If there is potential Strain and you didn't overreach, make a **Strain Resistance** test: **Resolve + Anima**. Each Hit cancels 1 Strain. Whatever's left is damage to the caster once the spell resolves. It ignores Armor and [[Sub-Attributes#Grit|Grit]], and you can't defend against it. A [[Wounds#Critical Wounds|Critical Wound]] from Strain uses the [[Critical Injuries#Strain Critical Injuries|Strain Critical Injuries]] table.

Strain is resolved on the spot and never tracked.

## Concentration

Some spells last only as long as the caster keeps them going. Each spell you're concentrating on gives **−5 to all your tests**, adding up: −10 for two, −15 for three, and so on. There's no limit beyond that. You can drop a spell at any time, as a Free Action.

Ordinary damage, including Strain, doesn't break Concentration. Only something serious does: when you take a [[Wounds#Critical Wounds|Critical Wound]] or become [[Conditions#Stunned|Stunned]], make a [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Composure]] check, with your Concentration penalty applied. On a failure, every spell you're concentrating on ends. Going [[Conditions#Unconscious|Unconscious]] or [[Death and Incapacitation|Incapacitated]] ends them automatically.
