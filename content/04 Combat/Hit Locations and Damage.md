
## Hit Locations

When an attack gets through the target's defenses, reverse the digits of the attack roll and compare the result to the Hit Location table.

For example, a roll of 34 becomes 43, a roll of 07 becomes 70, and a roll of 90 becomes 09. The reversed result is used only to determine Hit Location and does not affect the attack's success, [[Success Targets and Hits#Hits|Hits]], or damage.

| Roll | Hit Location |
|---|---|
| 01–10 | Head |
| 11–50 | Torso |
| 51–60 | Left Arm |
| 61–70 | Right Arm |
| 71–85 | Left Leg |
| 86–00 | Right Leg |

Groups that prefer not to use random Hit Locations may treat attacks as striking the torso by default. [[Combat Maneuvers#Called Shots|Called Shots]] may still target specific locations and are described later in this chapter.

## Damage

Once an attack gets through the target's defenses, add the attack's [[Opposed Tests#Net Hits|Net Hits]] to the weapon's flat Damage.

If the attack is a melee attack, also add the attacker's [[Sub-Attributes#Damage Modifier|Damage Modifier]].

Then apply Armor, Armor Penetration, and any other effects that modify damage before determining the [[Wounds|Wound]] inflicted. Wounds and their effects are described in [[Harm and Recovery]].
