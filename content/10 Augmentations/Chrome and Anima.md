## Anima Cost

Every augmentation has an **Anima Cost**. Installing it lowers the character's [[Anima]] by that amount, **for good**: removing the augmentation later doesn't bring the Anima back. Invasive or badly fitted augmentations cost more. Biological or custom-fitted ones cost less.

For the Awakened, every point counts. A Vessel can lose powers, and a Spellcaster's tests and Strain Resistance get worse (see [[Anima#Losing Anima|Losing Anima]]).

## Reaching 0 Anima

A character whose Anima reaches 0 through augmentation becomes a **Cyberzombie**. Their higher thought, their identity and their humanity are gone. Only instinct and basic drives are left. They're no longer a player character, and the GM takes over.

Nothing stops a player from installing the piece that takes them there. If that's how their character's story ends, it's their call.
