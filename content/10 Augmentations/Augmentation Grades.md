Augmentations aren't all made and fitted to the same standard. An augmentation's **Grade** changes its Price and its Anima Cost, not how well it works. Unless something says otherwise, an augmentation is **Standard**.

| Grade | Price | Anima Cost | What it is |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| **Bootleg** | ×½ | +50%, rounded up | Counterfeit, badly matched, crudely refurbished or fitted with no care |
| **Standard** | ×1 | As listed | Ordinary commercial ware, fitted to normal medical standards |
| **Tailored** | ×2 | −25%, rounded down | Built, grown or reworked for one specific person |

**Example:** a cybereye with an Anima Cost of 8 costs 12 Anima as Bootleg, and 6 as Tailored.

## Second-Hand Augmentations

An augmentation taken out of someone else wasn't made for you. Second-hand **[[Cyberware]]** counts as Bootleg unless it's rebuilt for its new owner. With the right facilities, parts and time, a [[Skills#Expertise Skills|Cyberware]] test brings it back to Standard. A Tailored augmentation never stays Tailored when it changes hands.

Second-hand **[[Bioware]]** usually can't be reused at all. When it can, preparing it takes [[Skills#Expertise Skills|Bioware]], [[Skills#Expertise Skills|Medicine]] or both, depending on the procedure.
