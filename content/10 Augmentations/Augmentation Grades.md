Not all augmentations are manufactured, fitted, or installed to the same standard.

An augmentation's **Grade** modifies both its Price and its Anima Cost. Higher-grade augmentations use better materials, cleaner interfaces, more extensive calibration, and greater customization to reduce the disruption caused by integrating them with the body. Lower-grade augmentations cut those corners.

Unless stated otherwise, augmentations are **Standard Grade**.

## Bootleg

**Bootleg** augmentations are counterfeit, poorly matched, crudely manufactured, improperly refurbished, or installed with minimal customization.

A Bootleg augmentation costs **50% of its listed Price** and **150% of its listed Anima Cost**.

Round the final Anima Cost to the nearest **0.25**.

Bootleg does not necessarily mean the augmentation is mechanically weaker. Its increased Anima Cost represents poor integration with the recipient rather than reduced performance.

## Standard

**Standard** augmentations represent normal commercial-grade ware manufactured and fitted according to contemporary medical and technical standards.

Standard augmentations use their listed **Price** and **Anima Cost** without modification.

Unless an augmentation entry states otherwise, it is assumed to be Standard Grade.

## Tailored

**Tailored** augmentations are built, cultured, or extensively adapted for a specific recipient. Their interfaces, dimensions, biological compatibility, calibration, and other integration requirements are customized to reduce the disruption caused by the augmentation.

A Tailored augmentation costs **200% of its listed Price** and **75% of its listed Anima Cost**.

Round the final Anima Cost to the nearest **0.25**.

Tailored Grade does not normally improve the augmentation's listed performance. Its primary advantage is reduced Anima Cost.

## Second-Hand Augmentations

Previously installed augmentations are not automatically compatible with a new recipient.

Second-hand **[[Cyberware]]** normally counts as **Bootleg** when installed in another character unless it has been professionally rebuilt and adapted for them.

With appropriate facilities, parts, time, and a **[[Skills#Expertise Skills|Cyberware]]** test, second-hand Cyberware may be restored to **Standard Grade** before installation. Tailored Grade cannot normally be preserved when an augmentation changes recipients.

Second-hand **[[Bioware]]** is rarely reusable. When transfer is possible, adapting or preparing it requires **[[Skills#Expertise Skills|Bioware]]**, **[[Skills#Expertise Skills|Medicine]]**, or both, depending on the procedure.

Unless an individual augmentation states otherwise, Bioware cannot be transferred directly from one character to another without specialized preparation.
