The Net connects nearly every part of modern life, from communication and identity to weapons, vehicles, security systems, infrastructure, and augmented reality. Most people interact with it constantly without thinking about the underlying systems that make those connections possible.

This chapter covers how characters connect to and interact with [[The Net]], how [[Access and Authority|access and authority]] are represented, and how [[Hacking|hackers]] bypass security, compromise devices and [[The Net#Hosts|Hosts]], deploy [[Hacking#Scripts|Scripts]], and manage the [[Hacking#Heat|Heat]] generated by illegal intrusion. It also covers [[Interfaces#Deep Interface|Deep Interface]] and the hardware used to access it.

[[The Net]] is not a separate virtual world. It is an interconnected layer of infrastructure embedded throughout the physical world. Hacking therefore happens alongside ordinary play, using the same [[Initiative and Action Points|action economy]] and [[02 Core Rules/index|core mechanics]] as other actions.
