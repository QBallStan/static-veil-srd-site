# Playtest Armor Catalogue

> [!warning] Playtest Draft
> This is a balancing worksheet, not the final equipment catalogue. It deliberately excludes prices, availability, manufacturers, modifications, coatings, and environmental systems.

## Catalogue Conventions

- A single armor item ranges from **Armor 0–10**.
- One [[Armor and Shields#Layering|Flexible]] item may layer beneath compatible outer armor. The absolute maximum combined Armor is **16**.
- No item covers every Hit Location. Body armor covers **Torso, Arms, and Legs** as listed; helmets remain separate so they can be removed independently.
- **Concealment** is the rating an armor item provides for hiding other equipment. It is not the armor's own [[Concealment#Bulk and Concealment|Bulk]].
- Only armor with meaningful pockets, folds, or internal storage receives a Concealment rating. Tactical and powered armor normally provide none.
- The listed Concealment ratings do not stack with other hiding places, following the normal concealment rules.

## Concealment Reference

| Concealment | Can reasonably hide |
|---:|---|
| 0 | Nothing beyond ordinary pockets. |
| 1 | Throwing knife, pistol magazine, small tool. |
| 2 | Holdout pistol, compact knife. |
| 3 | Light pistol, grenade, collapsed baton. |
| 4 | Heavy pistol, machine pistol, hand axe. |
| 5 | Compact SMG or personal-defense weapon. |

Armor-provided Concealment is capped at **5**. Full-size SMGs, swords, carbines, rifles, and similarly bulky equipment require a dedicated bag, compartment, vehicle, or other solution.
