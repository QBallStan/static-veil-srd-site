**Creatures** are beings driven by instinct rather than reason. They don't need the full range of learned Skills people have. A being that thinks, whatever its body or origin, uses the [[NPCs|NPC]] rules instead.

## Creature Skills

Creatures use this short list of Skills, rated 0 to 60 like any other Skill. The ratings cover whatever fits the creature: instinct, practice, things it has learned.

- **Athletics (Strength):** climbing, swimming, jumping, sprinting, grappling, throwing.
- **Combat (Agility):** natural weapons, and innate ranged attacks like thrown spines or fire breath. Throwing an object uses Athletics.
- **Dodge (Agility):** getting out of the way of attacks and sudden dangers.
- **Hunting (Instinct):** tracking, foraging, finding its way through its territory, finding and chasing prey.
- **Stealth (Agility):** hiding, stalking, approaching unseen.
- **Spellcasting (Anima):** deliberately casting spells. Only creatures that actually cast need it. An innate supernatural ability works through a [[Creature Traits|Trait]], a natural weapon or an [[Actions and Action Tables|Action]] instead.

For anything else, the creature makes the normal [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Attribute Check]].

A creature can also use Combat as its [[Active and Bonus Defense#Active Defense|Active Defense]] against a melee attack, if its body or natural weapons could plausibly block it. Otherwise, it has to Dodge.

## Health

Creatures have 12 Health and the normal [[Wounds|Wound penalties]]. An ordinary creature is out of the fight at 0, like a minor NPC (see [[Wounds#NPC Survival|NPC Survival]]). A major creature uses the full Critical Wound rules. For bodies that aren't humanoid, see [[Creature Hit Locations]].

## Behavior

Creatures don't fight like tacticians. They defend territory, hunt, feed, protect their young, run when they're hurt, or ignore their own survival altogether. They might react strongly to blood, noise, technology, magic or Anima. A line or two of behavior on the profile, plus [[Actions and Action Tables|Actions]] if they help, is enough to show it.
