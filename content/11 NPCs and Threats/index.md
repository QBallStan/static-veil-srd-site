Not everyone the crew runs into needs a full character sheet. A corporate guard needs a gun, the Skill to use it, and a point where he decides he isn't paid enough. The thing in the tunnels needs teeth and hunger. NPCs and creatures use the same rules as player characters. Their profiles just leave out whatever isn't going to come up.

Build what the scene needs. A bouncer who'll get shot at once needs three lines. A rival the crew will cross for the whole campaign can have a full sheet, with [[Actions and Action Tables|Actions]] and [[Creature Traits|Traits]] if they help.
