Traits describe what a character is, or how it always works: unusual movement, bodies, senses, protection, supernatural forms. They're most common on creatures, but anyone can have them if their nature justifies it. A one-off move made at a particular moment is an [[Actions and Action Tables|Action]] instead.

## Movement

Some movement Traits have a rating. Without one, the character uses its normal [[Sub-Attributes#Movement|Movement]] when moving that way. With one, it uses the rating instead: it replaces Movement, it doesn't add to it. Movement already spent in a round stays spent when you switch modes.

- **Amphibious:** breathes both air and water. It gives no swimming speed.
- **Aquatic [X]:** moves and acts underwater with no penalty, at its Movement or X. It says nothing about breathing.
- **Water-Breather:** breathes water, not air. Without Amphibious, it can't breathe out of water.
- **Burrow [X]:** moves through soil, sand and anything similar at its Movement or X, but not through stone, metal or reinforced structures. Fully underground, it has [[Cover and Shields#Cover|Full Cover]] from the surface and can't be targeted without a way to detect it and get through what's above it. Coming up unnoticed is Stealth against Perception. It leaves no usable tunnel, unless its profile says so.
- **Climber [X]:** climbs any surface it could plausibly grip at its Movement or X, with no Athletics test. A surface that's very unstable or slippery can still call for one.
- **Flight [X]:** flies at its Movement or X, moving up and down freely and ignoring ground terrain. In any round it stays in the air, it has to move at least half its Flight or land. If it goes [[Conditions#Prone|Prone]], is [[Conditions#Restrained|Restrained]] or otherwise can't stay up, it falls.
- **Hover:** stays in the air without moving and changes direction freely, so it ignores Flight's minimum. It needs Flight, unless the profile gives another way to stay up. It still falls if it goes Unconscious, unless its hovering is automatic.
- **Leaper [X]:** as part of its movement, it jumps up to half its Movement, or X meters, horizontally, and half that vertically. There's no Athletics test, and it ignores what's in between, but it has to land somewhere that can hold it. The jump counts against its Movement.
- **Wall-Crawler [X]:** moves across walls and ceilings at its Movement or X, even with no handholds, and needs no Athletics test to stay on. If something breaks its grip, it falls.

## Protection and Recovery

- **Natural Armor X:** Armor X over its whole body. It doesn't stack with worn armor: use the higher value. Armor Penetration and [[Damage Types and Effects#Direct Damage|Direct Damage]] work on it normally. It can't be taken off, but something that destroys the anatomy it protects can affect it.
- **Regeneration X:** at the start of its first turn each round, it recovers X Health, as long as it has at least 1. A profile can name what suppresses it, such as *Regeneration 2 (suppressed by Fire)*. Damage marked as non-regenerable is tracked separately and heals normally.
- **Elusive X:** especially hard to hit, whether because it's tiny, very fast or very hard to follow. Attacks against it take −10 × X. Area effects ignore this Trait.
- **Immune: X:** completely unaffected by X. Name it precisely: *Immune: Fire* stops Fire damage and its effects, while *Immune: Burning* stops the Burning condition but not the damage from the fire.
- **Resistant: X:** its [[Sub-Attributes#Grit|Grit]] is doubled against X.
- **Vulnerable: X:** it gets no Grit against X.

A character can only be one of Immune, Resistant or Vulnerable to the same thing.

## Senses and Concealment

- **Camouflage:** natural camouflage for an environment named on its profile: +20 to Stealth while still there, +10 while moving. It's no help anywhere else.
- **Echolocation:** perceives shapes, surfaces, movement and creatures in complete darkness by sound, but not color, writing, screens or anything else purely visual. [[Conditions#Deafened|Deafened]] affects it, and vacuum, soundproofing or interference can block it. It doesn't automatically beat Stealth.
- **Enhanced Sense: X:** +20 to Perception, tracking and locating when the named sense (hearing, smell, vibration, taste, electroreception…) is the main one being used, and it can actually provide the information.
- **Low-Light Vision:** ignores penalties for dim light, but not complete darkness.
- **Darkvision:** sees in complete darkness as if it were lit. It doesn't see through cover, reveal the invisible, or show anything normal sight couldn't.
- **Thermal Vision:** sees heat signatures in complete darkness and ignores darkness penalties against them. Insulation, strong ambient heat, thermal camouflage and heat-blocking barriers can hide things from it. It shows no color or writing.
- **Electromagnetic Vision:** perceives electrical currents and emissions: active electronics, live power lines, strong signals. Shielding, switched-off gear, interference and dense barriers can hide things from it. It isn't normal sight and doesn't see through walls.
- **Blind:** has no working vision, and automatically fails anything that needs sight. It's part of its body, not the [[Conditions#Blinded|Blinded]] condition, and its other senses may let it get by.

## Biological Hazards

- **Venomous: X:** when one of its designated attacks takes at least 1 Health off a living target, the target is exposed to venom X. The venom gives its own resistance test, onset, interval and effects. Simply touching the character isn't enough, unless the profile says otherwise.
- **Diseased: X:** the same, with disease X. Prolonged contact, handling or eating its remains, or spending time in a badly contaminated lair can also expose someone, at the GM's discretion. The disease gives its own resistance test, incubation, interval, symptoms and recovery.

## Supernatural Form

- **Incorporeal:** moves through creatures and non-magical solid objects, but can't stop inside one. Ordinary Kinetic damage can't hurt it. Magic, Direct Damage and anything specifically able to touch incorporeal beings work normally. It can't handle physical objects or make physical attacks, unless a weapon, Action or other Trait lets it.
