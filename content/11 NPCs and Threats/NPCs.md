NPCs use the same Attributes, Skills, tests and combat rules as player characters. Their profiles only list what's likely to come up.

## Skills

List only the Skills that matter, Core and Expertise together, grouped by rating so the profile is quick to read:

- **40:** Marksmanship
- **30:** Dodge, Hacking, Coding
- **20:** Athletics, Stealth, Electronics, Cyberware
- **10:** Melee

An unlisted Core Skill is at 0 and can be used as normal. An unlisted Expertise Skill isn't known. Give each NPC whatever ratings fit them: they don't have to follow character creation or advancement.

## Minor and Major NPCs

A **minor NPC** gets only what their role in the scene needs. They have 12 Health and the normal [[Wounds|Wound penalties]], and they're out of the fight as soon as they drop to 0 (see [[Wounds#NPC Survival|NPC Survival]]).

A **major NPC** uses the same full sheet and rules as a player character, Critical Wounds included. Use that for anyone whose injuries, resources or full range of abilities will matter.

## Action Points

NPCs and creatures have **3 AP** per round by default, like player characters. The GM can change that to fit the threat: 2 for a sluggish drone, more for something fast or monstrous.

## Running NPCs

NPCs who think act on what they know and what they want. Only roll for their behavior if that helps you run a particular NPC.

Don't play out fights between NPCs unless the result is uncertain and matters to the player characters. Similar minor NPCs can share one [[Initiative and Action Points#Initiative|Initiative]], and you can use any other shortcut that keeps the game moving. Any NPC can have [[Actions and Action Tables|Actions]] and [[Creature Traits|Traits]].
