Creatures use the same [[Hit Locations and Damage#Hit Locations|Hit Location rule]] as everyone else: reverse the digits of the attack roll. They just read the result on the table that matches their body. A creature that doesn't fit any of these uses the closest one, or has its own table on its profile. Groups who don't roll for locations hit the torso, or the creature's equivalent: Midbody, Body and so on.

These tables describe the shape of a body, not its size. Use common sense when an unusual body affects positioning, cover, grappling, being shoved, or whether an action is even possible.

## Humanoid

Humanoid Exogens, transformed creatures, ape-like beings and anything else with a roughly human layout of limbs use the [[Hit Locations and Damage#Hit Locations|standard table]].

## Quadruped

Wolves, big cats, deer-like Primals and anything else that walks on four legs.

| Roll | Hit Location |
|---|---|
| 01–10 | Head |
| 11–50 | Torso |
| 51–60 | Left Foreleg |
| 61–70 | Right Foreleg |
| 71–85 | Left Hindleg |
| 86–00 | Right Hindleg |

## Winged Quadruped

Dragons, gryphon-like beings and other four-legged creatures with a separate pair of wings. The extra limbs take some of the Torso's share.

| Roll | Hit Location |
|---|---|
| 01–10 | Head |
| 11–40 | Torso |
| 41–50 | Left Foreleg |
| 51–60 | Right Foreleg |
| 61–70 | Left Hindleg |
| 71–80 | Right Hindleg |
| 81–90 | Left Wing |
| 91–00 | Right Wing |

## Winged Biped

Large birds, harpy-like creatures and other two-legged creatures whose forelimbs are wings.

| Roll | Hit Location |
|---|---|
| 01–10 | Head |
| 11–50 | Torso |
| 51–60 | Left Wing |
| 61–70 | Right Wing |
| 71–85 | Left Leg |
| 86–00 | Right Leg |

## Serpentine

Giant snakes, wyrms, Naga-like creatures and anything else with a long body and no major limbs. The Forebody is the neck and the stretch just behind the Head.

| Roll | Hit Location |
|---|---|
| 01–10 | Head |
| 11–30 | Forebody |
| 31–70 | Midbody |
| 71–00 | Tail |

## Multi-Limbed

Spiders, insectoids, strange Fold organisms and anything else with too many similar limbs to list one by one. A Limb result hits whichever limb best fits where the attack came from.

| Roll | Hit Location |
|---|---|
| 01–10 | Head or Core |
| 11–50 | Torso or Abdomen |
| 51–00 | Limb |

A creature with several distinct kinds of limbs can split the Limb range between them on its profile.

## Amorphous

Slimes, clouds, masses of flesh, some spirits and anything else without stable limbs or a conventional body.

| Roll | Hit Location |
|---|---|
| 01–00 | Body |

A creature with a distinct Core uses 01–10 for the Core and 11–00 for the Body. A creature with no meaningful locations doesn't roll at all.

## Called Shots

You can make a [[Combat Maneuvers#Called Shots|Called Shot]] at any obvious body part, even on a creature without a Hit Location table. The GM decides whether it's there, whether it can be reached, and what hitting it does. A creature with no distinct anatomy may have nothing to aim at. Its profile should say if it has a core, an anchor or some other weak point.
