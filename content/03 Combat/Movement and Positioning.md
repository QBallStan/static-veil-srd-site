
## Movement

A character can move up to their [[Sub-Attributes#Movement|Movement]] in meters each round. They can split that distance across their turns, moving before, after or between actions, unless a rule or the situation prevents it. Moving normally doesn't cost AP.

Movement used during a round stays spent until the next round starts.

### Sprinting

A character can Sprint to go further than their normal Movement. Sprinting is a Minor Action that costs 1 AP. Make an [[Skills#Core Skills|Athletics]] test. Each [[Success Targets and Hits#Hits|Hit]] adds 2 meters to the character's Movement for the current round.

A character can Sprint more than once in the same round. Each Sprint gives a cumulative −5 to all their other tests for the rest of the round.

### Difficult Terrain

Mud, rubble, deep snow, shallow water and similar ground count as **Difficult Terrain**. Each meter moved through it costs 2 meters of Movement.

The GM can call for a test when the ground is especially unstable or dangerous, or rule that it can't be crossed without the right equipment or abilities.
