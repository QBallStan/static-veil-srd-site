
## Core Combat Exchange

Most attacks follow the same procedure:

1. The attacker makes an attack test.
2. If it succeeds, work out the attacker's total [[Success Targets and Hits#Hits|Hits]].
3. The defender rolls their defense:
    - Against a melee attack: an [[Active and Bonus Defense#Active Defense|Active Defense]], plus any Parry or shield dice as [[Active and Bonus Defense#Bonus Defense|Bonus Defense]].
    - Against a ranged attack: their [[Cover and Shields#Cover|cover dice]] as Bonus Defense, if they have any.
4. Compare the attacker's Hits to the defender's Defense Hits.
5. If the defender has at least as many Defense Hits as the attacker has Hits, the attack is stopped.
6. Otherwise, the attack lands, and the attacker's [[Opposed Tests#Net Hits|Net Hits]] add to its damage.
7. Determine the [[Hit Locations and Damage#Hit Locations|Hit Location]], then apply [[Active and Bonus Defense#Damage Reduction|Damage Reduction]]: Armor, Armor Penetration and the defender's [[Sub-Attributes#Grit|Grit]]. Subtract whatever damage is left from the target's [[Wounds|Health]].

## Making an Attack

An attack is a Major Action that costs 2 AP, unless a rule says otherwise.

Melee attacks, armed or unarmed, normally use Agility + Melee. [[Firearms]], bows, crossbows and similar weapons normally use Agility + Marksmanship. Thrown weapons, grenades and improvised projectiles normally use Strength + Athletics.

Apply any [[Modifiers|modifiers]] for range, visibility, position, Wounds, weapon traits and other circumstances, then roll the d100 together with the [[Bonus Dice]] from the weapon's Damage profile.

If the d100 fails, the attack misses. Ignore the Bonus Dice, and the defender doesn't need to roll unless a rule says otherwise.

If the d100 succeeds, work out its Hits as normal and add the Bonus Hits from the weapon's dice. That's the attack's total Hits. An attack can succeed with 0 total Hits. It still lands unless the defender scores at least 1 Defense Hit.

## One Attack per Turn

A character can make only one attack per turn. With enough AP, they can attack again on a later turn in the same round, in the next pass.
