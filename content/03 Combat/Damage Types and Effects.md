
Not every attack does harm the same way. Its **Damage Delivery** decides how it interacts with Armor, and an **Elemental Effect** adds a secondary effect, such as fire, cold, shock, corrosion, supernatural decay or psychic trauma. An attack can have both: a stun baton might deal Kinetic Electric damage, while a lightning spell deals Direct Electric damage.

## Kinetic Damage

Most physical attacks deal **Kinetic Damage**: bullets, blades, impacts, explosions and anything else that transfers force into the target. [[Active and Bonus Defense#Damage Reduction|Damage Reduction]] applies as normal.

## Direct Damage

**Direct Damage** gets past ordinary physical protection. Armor doesn't reduce it unless the Armor, or another effect, specifically protects against that source. [[Sub-Attributes#Grit|Grit]] and the rest of Damage Reduction still apply.

## Elemental Effects

Elemental Effects come on top of the attack's normal damage. They don't change whether it's Kinetic or Direct. Unless an effect says otherwise, it applies when the attack [[Active and Bonus Defense#Stopping an Attack|lands]], even if Damage Reduction brings the damage down to 0.

### Fire

When a Fire attack lands, roll 1d10 to see if the target catches fire. If the roll meets the threshold, the target gains [[Conditions#Burning|Burning]].

| Situation | Ignition Threshold | Examples |
|---|---:|---|
| Resistant | 9+ | Most combatants |
| Flammable | 7+ | Clothing, gear or surroundings that could catch |
| Very Flammable | 5+ | Exposed fuel, dry material, sustained flame contact |
| Extremely Flammable | 3+ | Soaked in accelerant or drenched in fuel |

### Cold

When a Cold attack lands, the target gains 1 stack of [[Conditions#Chilled|Chilled]].

### Electric

When an Electric attack lands, the target gains 1 stack of [[Conditions#Distracted|Distracted]].

### Acid

Acid is written as **Acid X**. When an Acid attack lands, the target gains X stacks of [[Conditions#Corroding|Corroding]]. Acid 2 applies 2 stacks.

### Necrotic

Necrotic damage is supernatural decay that resists healing. Health lost to Necrotic damage needs twice the normal number of [[Success Targets and Hits#Hits|Hits]] to heal. Mark those Health boxes with an N. When the character heals, all other lost Health comes back first, and the Necrotic boxes last.

### Psychic

When a Psychic attack deals damage, the target gains 1 stack of [[Conditions#Stunned|Stunned]]. Fear, hallucinations, compulsions, memory changes and other effects come from the specific ability that causes them.
