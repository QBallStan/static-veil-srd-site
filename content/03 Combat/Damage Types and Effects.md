
Not every attack delivers harm in the same way. **Damage Delivery** determines how an attack interacts with Armor, while **Elemental Effects** add secondary consequences such as fire, cold, electrical shock, corrosion, supernatural decay, or psychic trauma.

An attack may have both a Damage Delivery method and an Elemental Effect. For example, an electrical weapon may deal Kinetic Electric damage, while a lightning spell may deal Direct Electric damage.

## Kinetic Damage

Most physical attacks deal **Kinetic Damage**. Bullets, blades, impacts, explosions, and similar attacks transfer force into the target.

Kinetic Damage is reduced by Armor normally.

[[Sub-Attributes#Toughness|Toughness]] and other applicable defenses apply normally.

## Direct Damage

**Direct Damage** bypasses ordinary physical protection.

Armor does not reduce Direct Damage unless the Armor or another effect specifically provides protection against that source.

Toughness and other applicable defenses still apply normally.

## Elemental Effects

Elemental Effects are applied in addition to an attack's normal damage. They do not determine whether the attack deals Kinetic or Direct Damage.

Each Elemental Effect defines when its secondary effect occurs.

### Fire

Fire scorches, spreads, and can ignite exposed material.

When an attack with the Fire effect successfully hits, roll 1d10 to determine whether the target ignites.

| Situation | Ignition Threshold |
|---|---:|
| Resistant | 9+ |
| Flammable | 7+ |
| Very Flammable | 5+ |
| Extremely Flammable | 3+ |

**Resistant** is the default for most combatants.

**Flammable** applies when clothing, equipment, or surrounding material can reasonably catch fire.

**Very Flammable** represents exposed fuel, dry combustible material, or sustained flame contact.

**Extremely Flammable** represents targets soaked in accelerants, engulfed in fuel, or similarly easy to ignite.

If the ignition roll succeeds, the target gains the [[Conditions#Burning|Burning]] status.

Ignition occurs on a successful hit and does not require the attack to inflict a [[Wounds|Wound]].

### Cold

Cold attacks freeze, numb, and impair movement.

When a successful attack with the Cold effect is not stopped by [[Active and Passive Defense#Active Defense|Active Defense]], the target gains 1 stack of [[Conditions#Chilled|Chilled]].

[[Active and Passive Defense#Passive Defense|Passive Defense]] and Armor may still prevent the attack from causing a Wound, but they do not prevent the Chilled stack.

### Electric

Electric attacks disrupt the nervous system through electrical shock.

When a successful attack with the Electric effect is not stopped by Active Defense, the target gains 1 stack of [[Conditions#Distracted|Distracted]].

Passive Defense and Armor may still prevent the attack from causing a Wound, but they do not prevent the Distracted stack.

### Acid

Acid burns and corrodes whatever it remains in contact with.

Acid effects are written as **Acid X**, where X is the number of [[Conditions#Corroding|Corroding]] stacks applied when the attack is not stopped by Active Defense.

For example, Acid 2 applies 2 stacks of Corroding.

### Necrotic

Necrotic effects cause supernatural decay, withering, and damage that resists normal healing.

Damage inflicted by Necrotic damage require twice the normal number of [[Success Targets and Hits#Hits|Hits]] to heal.

You can mark necrotic health boxes with a "N".

### Psychic

Psychic effects directly disrupt thought, perception, and consciousness.

When an attack with the Psychic effect inflicts a Wound, the target gains 1 stack of [[Conditions#Stunned|Stunned]].

Any additional effects such as fear, hallucinations, compulsions, memory alteration, or loss of control are defined by the specific ability causing them.
