
## Hit Locations

When an attack lands, reverse the digits of the attack roll and find the result on the table. A 34 becomes 43, a 07 becomes 70, and a 90 becomes 09. The reversed number only decides where it hits. It doesn't change the attack's success, [[Success Targets and Hits#Hits|Hits]] or damage.

| Roll | Hit Location |
|---|---|
| 01–10 | Head |
| 11–50 | Torso |
| 51–60 | Left Arm |
| 61–70 | Right Arm |
| 71–85 | Left Leg |
| 86–00 | Right Leg |

Groups who'd rather not roll for locations can have every attack hit the torso. [[Combat Maneuvers#Called Shots|Called Shots]] can still aim for a specific location.

## Damage

Every weapon has a **flat Damage** value and a pool of [[Bonus Dice]]. The dice add to the attack's Hits. The flat Damage is what the attack deals once it lands.

When an attack lands, add the weapon's flat Damage to the attack's [[Opposed Tests#Net Hits|Net Hits]]. For a melee attack, also add the attacker's [[Sub-Attributes#Damage Modifier|Damage Modifier]]. Then apply [[Active and Bonus Defense#Damage Reduction|Damage Reduction]]: Armor, Armor Penetration, the target's [[Sub-Attributes#Grit|Grit]] and anything else that lowers damage. What's left is the **Final Damage**, which comes off the target's [[Wounds|Health]].
