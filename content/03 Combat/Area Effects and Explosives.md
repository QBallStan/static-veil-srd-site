
Some attacks hit an area instead of a single target: explosions, flamethrowers, magical effects and the like. The attack roll decides where the effect lands, not how hard it hits. Its [[Success Targets and Hits#Hits|Hits]] don't add to the damage unless a rule says so.

## Area of Effect Attacks

Each Area of Effect has one of two shapes:

- **Blast:** a circle centered on a point. The listed value is its diameter.
- **Beam:** a straight strip starting from the attacker, with a listed length and width.

Choose where to put the effect and which way it faces, then make the attack test: Marksmanship for a flamethrower, Athletics for a thrown grenade, or whatever fits. Measure range to the chosen point and apply range, visibility, [[Wounds]] and other [[Modifiers|modifiers]] as normal.

If the attack succeeds, everyone in the area is affected. Roll the effect's [[Bonus Dice]] once and use the same damage for every target. Targets get no Active Defense and no cover dice, but an aware target can use [[Interrupt Actions#Evade|Evade]] before the effect resolves. Partial cover gives nothing against an area effect, although [[Cover and Shields#Cover|Full Cover]] can block it completely. Work out cover from where the effect starts.

Each target then applies their own [[Active and Bonus Defense#Damage Reduction|Damage Reduction]]: Falloff, then Armor and Armor Penetration, then [[Sub-Attributes#Grit|Grit]]. Area effects hit the Torso unless an effect says otherwise.

## Explosive Devices

Grenades, rockets, mines and demolition charges work the same way. The attack or placement test decides where the device ends up. When it goes off, roll its Bonus Dice and add the Bonus Hits to its flat Damage. With no test tied to them, these dice always count.

## Damage Falloff

Some area effects weaken with distance and list a **Falloff** value. Reduce the damage by that amount for each meter between the target and the effect's origin. Falloff 1/m takes off 1 per meter, Falloff 2/m takes off 2. Damage can't go below 0. For a Blast, measure from the center. For a Beam, measure from where it starts.

## Deviation

When a Blast attack fails, it lands somewhere else. Roll 2d6 for direction and read it on the diagram below: 7 means it carried straight on past the target, and 2 or 12 means it fell back toward the attacker. Then roll for how many meters it drifts: 1d6 for a thrown device, 2d6 for a launched one. Resolve the effect from where it lands. A thrown or launched explosive that misses still goes off unless a rule or [[Complications|Complication]] says otherwise.
