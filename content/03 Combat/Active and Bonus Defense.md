Defense decides whether an attack is stopped. There are two kinds, and a defender adds them together to get their total **Defense Hits**.

## Active Defense

An **Active Defense** is a d100 roll: the defender uses Melee or Dodge to stop the attack. It's available against melee attacks, and against ranged attacks within 1 meter (see [[Ranged Combat#Close-Quarters Fire|Close-Quarters Fire]]). The defender has to know the attack is coming and be able to react. It costs no AP unless a rule says otherwise.

Melee covers blocking, parrying, binding the attacker's weapon and controlling distance. Dodge is getting out of the way. Defending against an armed attack barehanded is possible but risky; see [[Melee Combat#Guard|Guard]] and [[Melee Combat#Unarmed Combat|Unarmed Combat]].

If the d100 succeeds, work out its [[Success Targets and Hits#Hits|Hits]] as normal. If it fails, it scores 0 Hits.

## Bonus Defense

**Bonus Defense** is any defense that comes from [[Bonus Dice]]: [[Cover and Shields#Cover|cover dice]], [[Melee Combat#Parry|Parry dice]] and [[Cover and Shields#Shields|shield dice]]. Bonus Defense dice always count, whether or not the defender makes an Active Defense, and whether or not it succeeds. Roll them together with the Active Defense, or on their own when there isn't one.

Against most ranged attacks, the cover dice are the only defense. A defender with no cover rolls nothing and has no Defense Hits.

## Stopping an Attack

Add the Active Defense's Hits to the Bonus Defense's Hits. If the total is equal to or higher than the attacker's Hits, the attack is stopped. Otherwise, the attack **lands**, and the difference becomes its [[Opposed Tests#Net Hits|Net Hits]].

Only Defense Hits can stop a successful attack. A defender with no Defense Hits can't stop it, even if the attack scored 0 Hits.

## Damage Reduction

Once an attack lands, **Damage Reduction** lowers its damage: Armor (adjusted by Armor Penetration), Shield Armor, [[Sub-Attributes#Grit|Grit]] and, for area effects, Falloff.

Damage Reduction never stops an attack. Even if it brings the damage down to 0, the attack has still landed, so anything that happens when an attack lands still applies. It doesn't trigger [[Interrupt Actions#Counterstrike|Counterstrike]] or anything else that needs an attack to be stopped.
