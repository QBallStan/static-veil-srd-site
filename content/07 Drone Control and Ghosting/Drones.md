A drone isn't a vehicle. Nobody rides in it. It's a tool that moves, watches and, when a human authorizes it, shoots. In play, it works like a very minor character with a short profile.

> **Hornet:** Small rotor drone · Autopilot 4 · Sensors 3 · Targeting 3 · Armor 2 · Senses: Low-Light Vision, Thermal Vision · Mount: drone SMG.

## Ratings

A drone has three ratings from 1 to 8. When it acts on its own or under orders, its Success Target is **rating × 10**:
- **Autopilot:** moving, maneuvering, avoiding obstacles, and its [[Active and Bonus Defense#Active Defense|Active Defense]] against melee attacks.
- **Sensors:** how well it perceives, for anything Perception would cover. Its **senses** are the [[Creature Traits#Senses and Concealment|Traits]] that say *what* it can perceive.
- **Targeting:** attacking with its weapons.

## Size

| Size | Examples | Health | Frame | Integrity | Mod slots | Traits |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Micro** | Spy drone, insect bot | 6 | 0 | 1 | 0 | Elusive 2 |
| **Small** | Courier drone, recon quadcopter | 12 | 1 | 2 | 1 | Elusive 1 |
| **Medium** | Combat drone, security crawler | 12 | 2 | 3 | 2 | — |
| **Large** | Heavy assault drone, cargo walker | 12 | 3 | 3 | 3 | — |

Flying drones tend to be harder to hit, and ground drones tend to be better armored. Most drones are easy targets compared with a person: what they lack in mobility, they make up for in how much punishment they can take.

## Damage

Drones take damage like characters: [[Active and Bonus Defense#Damage Reduction|Armor]], then **Frame**, which works like [[Sub-Attributes#Grit|Grit]], and the rest comes off their Health, with the same [[Wounds|Wound penalties]]: one step for every 3 points missing. A drone is a device too, so it can be [[Device Damage|Glitched, Disabled or Bricked]] by hacking.

At **0 Health**, the drone is **Disabled** and needs repair. It fills one **Integrity Slot** and rolls on the Critical Damage table: 1d100, **+5 for each point of damage past 0** and **+10 for each Critical Damage it already has**.

| d100 | Critical Damage |
|---|---|
| 01–20 | **Shaken loose:** nothing that a repair won't fix. |
| 21–35 | **Damaged sensor:** loses one of its senses. |
| 36–50 | **Jammed mount:** its weapon can't fire. |
| 51–65 | **Warped chassis:** Frame −1. |
| 66–75 | **Damaged motor:** Autopilot −1. |
| 76–85 | **Fried optics:** Sensors −1. |
| 86–95 | **Misaligned targeting:** Targeting −1. |
| 96–00 | **Total loss:** the drone is destroyed, and all that's left is salvage. |

The effect lasts until the damaged part is replaced. With every Integrity Slot filled, the next Critical Damage destroys it.

## Repairs and Parts

Each hour spent on a damaged drone allows a **Mechanics** or **Electronics** test. Each Hit restores 1 Health. Unlike a patient, a drone can be worked on for as long as it takes, so a few hours in the workshop usually gets it back to full. Drones don't recover on their own.

**Replacing a part** removes one Critical Damage, its effect and the Integrity Slot it filled, for about **10% of the drone's price** in parts, and a repair test. A Jockey who does the work themselves only pays for the parts.

## Weapons and Mods

Drone weapons are built for drones, and you can't mount an ordinary gun on one. They use the normal [[Weapons|weapon profile]], with no Recoil Modifier and no Minimum Strength, since the mount handles both. A drone **can't reload in the field**: someone has to swap its ammunition module (1 AP, within reach), or it has to return to its dock or vehicle.

**Mods** go in the drone's mod slots and list their requirements, like [[Armor Modding|armor mods]]: plating, an extra sense, a manipulator arm, a second mount. The GM decides how long they take to install.
