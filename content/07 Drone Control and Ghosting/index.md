In Static Veil, machines do not need hands on controls to move through the world.

Drones patrol corporate campuses, carry freight through crowded streets, inspect infrastructure, shadow convoys, scout hostile ground, and watch from places people cannot safely reach. Most can navigate, follow instructions, avoid hazards, and manage routine tasks without supervision. What they cannot normally do is make the decision to kill. Lethal force still requires an operator to authorize each engagement, a restriction born less from ethics than liability.

Some operators work at a distance through a **Drone Rig**, managing telemetry, issuing commands, coordinating multiple units, and running specialized Scripts. Others go further.

A **Somatic Link**, commonly called a **Ghostwire**, allows a user to project their senses and motor control directly into a vehicle or drone. While **Ghosting**, cameras become eyes, microphones become ears, engines become muscle, and the machine responds as naturally as the operator's own body.

The rules in this section cover commanding drones, coordinating drone squads, using Drone Rigs, autonomous systems, and Ghosting [[Operating Vehicles|vehicles]] or drones through a Somatic Link.
