These are baselines for getting things done without slowing the game down: what a character can do without rolling, and what a good test adds. They aren't limits. The GM can always adjust them for the situation.

## Lifting and Carrying

A character can **lift** up to **Strength AR × 30 kg** (minimum 15 kg): pick it up, move it a few steps, or hold it for a few seconds. An **Athletics** test adds **10 kg per Hit**. They can **carry** a third of that for as long as they keep walking, and push or drag about twice as much on a smooth surface.

**Example:** with Strength 34 (AR 3), Rook lifts 90 kg and carries 30 kg all day. To haul an unconscious troll of about 120 kg out of the line of fire, he needs an Athletics test with at least 3 Hits.

## Jumping

With a run-up of at least 2 m, a character jumps **Strength AR in meters** without rolling. An **Athletics** test adds **0.5 m per Hit**. A standing jump goes half as far, and a high jump reaches a quarter of that distance. The jump counts against Movement.

Someone who falls short by 1 m or less can grab the edge with a [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Grip]] check. Otherwise, they [[Hazards#Falling|fall]].

**Example:** a 4 m gap between two roofs. An ordinary adult (AR 3) clears 3 m without rolling, so they need 2 Hits.

## Running

[[Sub-Attributes#Movement|Movement]] is how far you can go in a fight while staying ready to act. Over distance, a character walks at about **5 km/h** and runs at about **12 km/h**. For each hour of running or other hard physical effort, they make a [[Skills#Attribute Checks|Vigor]] check. On a failure, they gain 1 [[Conditions#Fatigue|Fatigue]].

## Climbing and Swimming

A character climbs and swims at **half their Movement**. A ladder or calm water needs no test. A smooth wall, a strong current or rough water calls for an **Athletics** test. On a failure, they make no progress, and a [[Complications|Complication]] can mean a fall or going under. A load heavier than what they can carry, or Restrictive armor, makes it harder, or impossible.
