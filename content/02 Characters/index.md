A character in Static Veil is more than a collection of trained abilities. Flesh can be replaced. Reflexes can be accelerated. Memories can be altered, senses sharpened, and the body pushed past anything it was born able to do.

Magic blurs the line even further. Some people can reach into forces most can barely perceive, drawing on something deeper than muscle, training or machinery. Everyone has [[Attributes#Anima|Anima]], the part of a person that holds body, mind and self together. For a few, it's a source of supernatural power. For everyone else, it's a limit: how much of themselves they can replace before something essential starts to give way.

For all that, a character is still defined by what they can do when the pressure is on. [[Attributes]] are what they were born with and [[Skills]] are what they've learned. Everything else, from [[Lineages|Lineage]] to [[10 Augmentations/index|augmentation]], builds on top of that. [[Traits]] add the strengths and flaws that make them a person, not just a set of numbers.
